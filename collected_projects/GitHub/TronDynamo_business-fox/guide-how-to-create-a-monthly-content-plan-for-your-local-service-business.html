How to Create a Monthly Content Plan for Your Local Service Business

Consistency is the hardest part of local marketing. When you run a service business—whether you do plumbing, landscaping, or accounting—marketing usually happens late at night when the real work is done. A monthly content bundle solves this by letting you plan, write, and schedule everything in one sitting.

Here is a practical, four-step system to build and publish a month of content without losing your weekends.

Step 1: Audit Your Common Customer Questions Start with what people actually ask you on the phone or at the job site. Do not guess what content to write. Look at your last twenty emails or text messages.

Write down questions like: - What does this service cost? - How long does the process take? - What do I need to prepare before you arrive? - What happens if something goes wrong?

Each of these questions is a distinct piece of content. A customer asking about preparation time is ready for a short checklist post. A question about cost is ready for a transparent pricing breakdown.

Step 2: Choose Your Core Formats Do not try to be on every platform. Pick three channels you can maintain: - One weekly blog post or FAQ update on your website. - Two social media posts per week featuring actual work photos or short tips. - One monthly email to past clients to stay top of mind.

Keep the format simple. Local service businesses win on trust and clarity, not fancy design.

Step 3: Block Two Hours for Batch Creation Do not write content every day. It interrupts your actual work. Block a single two-week or monthly window—such as two hours on a quiet Tuesday morning—to draft everything.

- Week 1 draft: Write four short answers to common questions. - Week 2 draft: Select four photos from recent jobs and write two sentences of context for each. - Week 3 draft: Write a short update about seasonal maintenance relevant to your local area.

Step 4: Schedule and Automate Use a basic content calendar or spreadsheet to load your posts into a scheduler. Spend thirty minutes on the last day of the month lining everything up. Once it is scheduled, close the tab and get back to serving your clients.
