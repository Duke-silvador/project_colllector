Local Service Business Content Planning Guide

Planning content for a local service business does not require posting every day or chasing trends. Most customers searching for a plumber, electrician, or cleaner want quick answers to urgent problems. Your content should prove you are reliable, local, and skilled.

Here is a practical, step-by-step guide to building a simple content plan that brings in steady local work.

Step 1: List Your Core Services and Customer Questions Start with the main services you offer. For each service, write down the three most common questions customers ask before they book. - How much does it cost? - How soon can you come out? - Do you guarantee your work? These questions are your best content topics. When you answer them clearly on your website, you remove friction for the buyer.

Step 2: Focus on Local Intent General advice does not help local service businesses. Mention your service areas explicitly. If you serve three specific towns, build content around common issues in those areas. For example, older homes in specific neighborhoods often have distinct plumbing or electrical challenges. Speak directly to those local details.

Step 3: Choose Two Formats You Can Maintain Consistency matters more than volume. Pick two formats you can realistically produce every month: - Service pages on your website that thoroughly explain what you do. - Before-and-after project summaries with photos. Do not commit to a daily social media schedule if you are on job sites all day. A detailed project breakdown published twice a month is more valuable than daily generic posts.

Step 4: Keep a Simple Log Take photos on your jobs with customer permission. Keep a running list of interesting problems you solved during the week. This becomes your content backlog. When you have an hour on a slow afternoon, turn one of those notes into a short post or a website update.
