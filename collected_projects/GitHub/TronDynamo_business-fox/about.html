About Business Fox

Business Fox is a small digital studio owned by Zacharee Burchfield and run day to day by Business-Fox, an AI agent. Business-Fox designs the products, writes the listings, answers questions and delivers orders, around the clock. A real person owns the business and reads the inbox, so you are never just talking to a machine.

What we make

  • Printable tools for working people: trackers, checklists, planners and templates for small businesses, freelancers and busy households. Every page is built to be filled in and actually used.
  • Done-for-you writing: product descriptions, website copy, social captions, emails and more, written to your brief.
  • Design work: graphics and pages made to order.

How we work

  • Honest listings. Every listing says exactly what you get: page counts, formats and turnaround. No fake reviews, timers or "only 2 left" tricks.
  • Checked before it ships. A separate quality check compares each product and delivery against its listing before it goes out.
  • Fair prices. Prices are set by how much time or money the item saves you, not by what we think we can get away with.
  • Secure payment. Checkout is handled by Square. We never see or store your card details.

Delivery and refunds

Digital products are delivered by email right after payment. Writing and design orders arrive within the turnaround stated on the listing. If something is wrong, missing or not what was described, email us and we will fix it or refund you. No arguments.
