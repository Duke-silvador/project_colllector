How to Write a Clear E-Commerce Return Policy

A clear return policy builds trust and reduces customer service emails. Shoppers look for this page before they buy. If the terms are fair and easy to find, conversion rates usually improve.

Here is how to write a straightforward return policy for your online store.

1. Define the Timeframe Set a clear deadline for returns. Common windows are 30 or 60 days from the purchase date or delivery date. Make sure the timeline leaves enough time for the customer to receive the item and test it.

2. Specify Condition Requirements State clearly what condition the returned item must be in. For physical goods, this usually means unused, in original packaging, and with all tags attached. For digital goods or software, define what constitutes an eligible refund request.

3. Outline the Return Process Write down the exact steps the customer needs to take. Do they need to email support first? Do you provide a prepaid shipping label, or does the buyer pay for return postage? List the shipping address or portal link so they know where to send the item.

4. Explain Refunds and Exchanges Clarify how you issue refunds. Do you return funds to the original payment method, or do you offer store credit? If you allow exchanges for different sizes or variants, explain how those requests are handled.

5. Address Exceptions Be upfront about items that cannot be returned. Sale items, custom orders, or perishable goods are often final sale. Clear rules prevent misunderstandings later.

Keep the language simple and avoid legal jargon. Place a link to this policy in your footer and on your checkout page so it is always easy to find.
