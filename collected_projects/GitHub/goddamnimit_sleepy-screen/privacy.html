Privacy policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Sleepy Screen does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it makes no network requests of its own. Everything you create stays on your device.

What the app stores on your device

Your Spaces, launcher items, themes, settings and (optionally) an imported home-screen screenshot are saved in the app's private storage and a shared app container used only by the app's own widgets. This data never leaves your device. It is included in your normal iPhone backups at your discretion, and deleting the app deletes it.

Permissions the app may ask for

  • Calendars (optional): lets the Top widget show your next event. Events are read on your device to display them and are not stored or sent anywhere.
  • Photos, add-only (optional): lets the app save a matching wallpaper image you create. The app cannot read your photo library for this. If you import a home-screen screenshot with the photo picker, only the image you choose is used, and it stays on your device.

Opening other apps

When you tap an item, the app asks iOS to open that app, link, Shortcut or phone number. Sleepy Screen does not see what happens next, and the other app's own privacy policy applies there.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
