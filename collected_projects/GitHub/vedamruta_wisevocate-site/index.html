How it works

It waits for the whole thought.

People pause, restart and change their mind mid-sentence. Wisevocate lets each speaker finish before it reads the turn, so the card is about what they actually said.

1

Captions on

Join your Teams call. Wisevocate asks once, then turns on live captions for you each time.

2

Speaker finishes

It waits until the person stops talking. One speaker's turn becomes one card, never merged with the next.

3

Copilot reads it

Your company's Copilot agent reads the turn in context and decides if it needs a card at all.

4

You answer

Read the likely meaning, pick a reply that sounds like you, and say it in your own words.

Short pauses inside a turn don't count. The card arrives after the speaker is done, just before it's your turn to talk. Your own words never get a card.

When it speaks up

Fewer cards. Better ones.

A normal question doesn't need help. Wisevocate shows a card only when a turn is assertive or confusing: pressure, a dig dressed up as a question, sarcasm, an idiom with a hidden meaning. Copilot judges the tone and the context, not a list of trigger words.

Inside a card

The meaning, and a way through.

What they may mean

One or two plain sentences on the intent and the concern underneath. Written with "may" and "seems", because nobody can read minds.

How sure, and what's at stake

A confidence level with a short reason, and a risk flag for what could go wrong if the answer lands badly.

Three ways to answer

Calm, honest and first-person. No made-up dates or promises, no quoting people back at them, nothing defensive.

Privacy

When the meeting ends, so does the memory.

At the end you get notes written from the whole meeting: summary, decisions, actions, open questions. Then the captions and cards are gone.

Microsoft Copilot is the only AI

Turns go to your company's own Copilot Studio agent, inside your Microsoft tenant. No other AI service is involved.

Nothing saved after the call

Captions, cards and notes live only for the meeting. Only your settings are kept on your PC.

It never speaks for you

Wisevocate doesn't talk, type or post in the meeting. Every word you say is your choice.

Built on PTC values

Every reply follows five values.

Customers Matter

Listen first and answer the concern underneath the words.

Team PTC

Build trust. Never blame a colleague, and keep promises realistic.

Be Bold

Disagree calmly. Saying "I don't know yet" is allowed.

PTC for All

Respectful and kind. Never mock, label or diagnose anyone.

Purposeful Innovation

Useful in the moment. Short and practical beats clever.
