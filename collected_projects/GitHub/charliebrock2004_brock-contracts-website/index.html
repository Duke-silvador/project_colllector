    How we work

    Straightforward, from the first call to the finish.

    Most of the work we take on is local. Being based in Crieff means we know the housing stock, the suppliers and the building warrant process with Perth & Kinross Council.

    1. Get in touch

      Call or email with what you have in mind. We’re happy to talk through any job, big or small.

    2. A clear quote

      A straightforward, no-obligation quote, so you know where you stand before any work starts.

    3. The work, managed

      As principal contractor or sub-contractor, we coordinate every trade on site so the job runs to programme.

    4. Finished properly

      Because we’re joiners as well as builders, the same firm takes care of the structure and the finishing work.
