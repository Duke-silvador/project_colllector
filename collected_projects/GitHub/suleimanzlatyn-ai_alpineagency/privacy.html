Privacy notice

This page describes general privacy considerations for visitors to the Alpine Agencies website. The business owner should review and adapt it to match actual data practices before publication.

Important: The current project enquiry form prepares an email in the visitor's email application. It does not submit information to a website database. If forms, analytics, tracking or third-party services are added later, this notice must be updated.

Information you choose to send

If you contact Alpine Agencies by email, telephone or WhatsApp, information you provide may be used to respond to your enquiry and discuss a potential engagement. Avoid sending sensitive personal information or confidential project material through an unapproved channel.

Third-party services

The website may load externally hosted fonts, images or links to third-party services. Those providers may process technical information according to their own privacy policies.

Data retention and your choices

Ask Alpine Agencies directly how enquiry correspondence is handled, how long it is retained and how to request correction or deletion where applicable.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the business using its verified official contact details.
