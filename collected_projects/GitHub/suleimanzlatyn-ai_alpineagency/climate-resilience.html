Climate Risk & Resilience

Consider how climate-related hazards and changing environmental conditions may affect sites, operations and long-term plans — then identify practical priorities for resilience.

Discuss this service ↗All services
Plan for change

Make climate considerations part of environmental risk planning.

Climate risk work starts with the location, activity, assets and decisions at stake. Alpine can help frame the questions, identify information gaps and scope proportionate next steps for a site or operation.

  • Identify relevant climate and environmental hazards
  • Consider exposure, vulnerability and operational consequences
  • Prioritise adaptation options and risk-reduction measures
  • Connect resilience planning with environmental management
Potential scope

From initial review to a practical action plan.

The appropriate scope depends on the site, available information, the project requirements and any specialist studies needed.

01 / CONTEXT

Understand exposure

Review the site setting, activities, assets and relevant climate-related concerns.

02 / PRIORITIES

Assess vulnerability

Identify likely sensitivities, information gaps and areas requiring closer assessment.

03 / OPTIONS

Consider adaptation

Outline practical measures for further evaluation and risk reduction.

04 / ACTION

Define next steps

Set out proposed actions, responsibilities and monitoring considerations.

What a project may cover

  • Climate and environmental hazard screening
  • Site or operational vulnerability considerations
  • Resilience opportunities and adaptation options
  • Integration with environmental management and incident preparedness
  • Recommendations for further technical assessment where required

Clear, proportionate recommendations

Recommendations should reflect the available evidence and the actual needs of the project. Where modelling, engineering design, specialist measurements or regulated assessments are required, these should be scoped with appropriately qualified specialists.
