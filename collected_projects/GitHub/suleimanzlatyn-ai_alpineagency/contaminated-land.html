Contaminated Land Management

Understand potential land contamination, assess environmental risk and plan proportionate investigation, remediation and verification discussions for your site.

Discuss this service ↗All services
Understand the site

Start with the site history, risks and intended use.

Land contamination concerns can arise from previous activities, storage areas, spills, waste handling or unknown site conditions. A sensible first step is to clarify the site context, available records, potential receptors and the decisions the assessment needs to support.

  • Review the site setting, history and known environmental concerns
  • Identify potential contaminant sources and pathways for further assessment
  • Scope appropriate investigation with qualified specialists
  • Consider remediation options and verification requirements
Potential project steps

Move from concern to a defined scope of work.

Every site is different. The required approach depends on site conditions, intended use, applicable requirements and the evidence available.

01 / CONTEXT

Review the site

Gather available site history, activities, constraints and known concerns.

02 / RISK

Identify questions

Consider potential sources, pathways and receptors requiring investigation.

03 / SCOPE

Plan assessment

Agree appropriate specialist input, investigation objectives and deliverables.

04 / FOLLOW-UP

Review options

Consider remediation, verification and ongoing management needs where applicable.

What support may include

  • Initial review of site context and available records
  • Identification of environmental risk questions and information gaps
  • Scoping discussions for sampling, testing and specialist investigation
  • Review of remediation approaches and follow-up requirements
  • Clear reporting of recommendations and proposed next steps

Evidence-led decisions

Investigation, laboratory analysis, regulated assessments and remediation design should be carried out by appropriately qualified and authorised professionals as required. The scope, credentials and deliverables for any engagement should be confirmed before work begins.
