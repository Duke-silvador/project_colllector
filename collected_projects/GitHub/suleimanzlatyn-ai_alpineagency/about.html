About Alpine

Environmental risk reduction, with practical action in mind.

Alpine Environmental Risk Reduction Agency is positioned as an environmental consultancy focused on helping organisations prevent and mitigate pollution and adapt to climate change.

Our purpose

Help clients make better-informed environmental decisions.

We focus on understanding environmental concerns, clarifying requirements and identifying practical next steps. From compliance and water monitoring to incident management and contaminated land, each engagement starts with the specific site and the problem to be solved.

  • Prevent pollution where risks can be anticipated
  • Mitigate impacts when environmental incidents occur
  • Use monitoring and evidence to guide decisions
  • Strengthen resilience to climate-related risks
