If there is an incident

Prioritise people and immediate safety.

  • Follow your site emergency plan and contact local emergency services where required.
  • Keep people away from the hazard and do not enter unsafe areas.
  • Only take actions you are trained and authorised to perform.
  • When safe, communicate the location, material or asset involved, and known risks.
  • Preserve relevant site information for responders.

Do not delay emergency services while trying to reach Alpine Agencies. Response availability and dispatch timing must be confirmed directly.
