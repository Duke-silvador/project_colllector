Buyer contract tracking worksheet

Track Your Purchase from Contract to Closing

As a buyer, enter the effective date and closing date from your signed contract. Choose a task from each dropdown, then enter the exact period stated in your agreement—or type an agreed due date directly. The timeline below updates automatically whenever you add, remove, edit or move a task.

Start with a few rows. Open What needs to happen? to choose from the full checklist of contract-related tasks, or choose Other task to type your own. Use + Add blank task for more rows. The template suggests task names only—it does not set contractual deadlines. Sample day counts are shown in green as examples from a prior worksheet—not contractual deadlines. Enter and confirm the actual number of days from your signed agreement before relying on any calculated date. Calendar-day calculations do not apply legal weekend, holiday, or notice-delivery rules; business-day calculations skip weekends only, not holidays.
