01 · THE SUM

Six lines. Everything else is detail.

1

MSD01 is a flat, powerbank-sized device that clips to a phone and gives it eighteen medical, sleep and environmental senses a phone does not have.

2

It runs thirteen AI models on its own silicon. Raw signal becomes an answer a person understands — stress, sleep, breathing, cough, tremor, fever, glucose trend, and the health of the room they live in.

3

It has no screen. The phone is the screen, and the app installs it in under a minute. With no phone in range it keeps working and sends over its own eSIM.

4

It is the first physical sensor input into Audere's Self-CAV and Coach Mpilo WhatsApp companions — giving an AI that already talks to millions of people a body and a room to listen to.

5

It is built for 2030, not for 2026: 5G RedCap and NTN on the roadmap, iSIM, post-quantum signing, a digital product passport, a user-replaceable battery and a housing that comes apart in five minutes with no adhesive.

6

Phase 1 is three units — one bench, one field, one to Audere — sixteen weeks, one clinic and ten households in Gauteng.

02 · THREE ASPECTS

Three things that are usually three products.

Sensors, on-device intelligence and the care conversation — fused into one object a person can put in a pocket.

Aspect I

Sensors

  • 18 channels on two boards — the sensor board faces the person, the main board faces the phone
  • 60 GHz radar reads heart and breathing through a blanket, no contact
  • Three channels on the same two skin pads: EDA, ECG, hydration
  • The room as a sensor: CO₂, PM2.5, VOC, CO, NO₂, light, UV
MR60BHA2 · MAX86141 · MAX30001 · MLX90632 · AMG8833 · SCD41 · BMV080
Aspect II

Local processor & integrated AI

  • Two brains: the Walter SoM runs the device, the Himax WiseEye2 NPU runs the eyes
  • 13 models — 11 in the signal domain, 2 in the frame domain
  • Camera frames and audio are consumed in RAM and never stored, never sent
  • An on-device MCP server any agent can discover — seven tools, nothing hidden
ESP32-S3 + Sequans GM02SP · Cortex-M55 + Ethos-U55 · 9.8 µA deep sleep
Aspect III

Software — Audere Self-CAV

  • Self-CAV and Coach Mpilo already talk to people at scale, on WhatsApp, in their own language
  • MSD01 gives that conversation measured truth instead of a memory
  • Signed CBOR records · FHIR R4 export · POPIA release gate, a human in the loop
  • Nothing leaves the device until the person says so

06 · THE APP IS THE SCREEN

Pair. Check in. Release.

Three screens. The device installs from the app in under a minute; the release step is the POPIA gate — explicit, per record, revocable.

1 · Pair

The phone finds the device, names it, and installs it. No account needed to start.

2 · Check in

Forty seconds. Heart, breathing, temperature, skin, cough — and the room alongside.

3 · Release

The record is signed on the device. It goes to Self-CAV only when the person releases it.

08 · WHAT IT DOES NOT CLAIM

The honest limits, stated first.

MSD01 is not a registered medical device. No SAHPRA registration, no clinical claim — and there will be none until there is.
The glucose channel is research-grade. Per-person calibration, a direction rather than a number, and no accuracy figure committed before one is earned.
Apnoea and seizure outputs are screening signals. They say seek assessment. They do not say you have this.
Cuffless blood pressure is not in this build and will not be claimed without a validated study.
Accuracy figures in the planning document are design targets. The bench unit exists to replace them with measured ones.
Nothing on this page is medical, legal or financial advice.
