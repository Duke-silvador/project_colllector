05 — NEED TO KNOW

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Good questions deserve clear answers.

Read the basics before creating an account.

Is campaign access automatic after registration?

No. Accounts remain pending until the required registration details and payment reference have been reviewed by an administrator.

Are earnings guaranteed?

No. There is no guaranteed income. Campaign availability, eligibility, rates and approval conditions vary and must be shown in the specific campaign brief.

Does submitting a screenshot guarantee approval?

No. Screenshots are evidence for review, not automatic proof of genuine reach or engagement. An administrator checks each submission.

How long does a campaign run?

Each campaign displays its own start and end time. Some campaigns may use a 20-hour participation window, as stated in their brief.

How do I get help with my account?

Sign in and use the contact or support instructions published by the agency. Never share your password or one-time verification codes.
