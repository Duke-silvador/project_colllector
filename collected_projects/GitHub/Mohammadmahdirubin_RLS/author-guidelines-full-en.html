Russian Language Studies

Author Guidelines

1. Scope and suitability

Manuscripts must relate to Russian language, RFL teaching, linguistics, Russian literature and culture, translation, intercultural communication, comparative and interdisciplinary studies. Accepted article types: Research Article and Review Article.

2. Originality and submission

  • Original work only; not previously published or under review elsewhere.
  • Accurate documentation of all sources.
  • Submission implies acceptance of the journal’s ethics and peer-review policies.

3. Required files

  • Main manuscript: without author identifiers (anonymous review).
  • Author details file: names, affiliation, email, ORCID if any.
  • Declarations: originality, conflict of interest, as announced.

4. Authors and corresponding author

Author order is fixed at submission. The corresponding author handles correspondence and confirms co-author consent.

5. Suggested structure

Title (Persian & English), abstracts and keywords, introduction, aims, literature review, framework, method, findings, discussion, conclusion, funding, conflict of interest, AI statement if used, acknowledgements, references.

6. Title, abstract, keywords

Clear concise title; abstracts ≈150–250 words; 4–7 keywords.

7. Formatting

Editable Word file; consistent orthography; numbered tables/figures referred to in the text.

8. Referencing

APA 7th edition. In-text citations must match the reference list. Include valid DOIs where available.

9–12. Data, thesis work, funding, AI

Tables/figures must be necessary and sourced. Declare thesis/project origin if applicable. Declare funding and conflicts of interest. Declare use of generative AI; authors remain fully responsible.

13. Evaluation

Initial screening for scope, originality and completeness, then specialist review.

14. Pre-submission checklist

  • Scope match; originality; anonymized main file; separate author file
  • Abstracts/keywords checked; citations complete; DOIs verified
  • Funding/COI/AI declared; all authors approved
