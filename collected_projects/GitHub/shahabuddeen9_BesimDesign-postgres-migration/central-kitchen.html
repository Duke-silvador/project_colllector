Pillars of Commissary Engineering

Every square foot is engineered for zero-contamination, heavy thermal duty, and regulatory compliance.

Cleanroom Hygiene Zoning

Progressive positive air-pressure cascades isolating raw receiving from cooked packing suites. Includes touchless air shower locks, antibacterial epoxy coving, and sanitary gowning entry.

ISO Clean Air Cascades

Sub-Zero Blast Freezers (-40°C)

Deep-freeze batch crystallization units delivering rapid core pulldown from +90°C to -18°C within 90 minutes. Thermal-break insulated sub-floors prevent permafrost slab heaving.
