Privacy & limitations

Where your records go

The two free tools save your project or practice information in your own browser using localStorage. We do not currently offer user accounts, cloud sync, external AI processing, form submissions, or third-party analytics in these applications. This means we do not receive your client entries, notes, prices, or practice statistics through the tools.

Backups and deletion

Use the Export JSON buttons to create downloadable backups. You can clear your local data with the in-app Clear button or by deleting your browser's site storage. Clearing browser storage, using private browsing, or switching devices can erase records. We cannot recover them because we do not host them.

Hosting

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process standard web traffic metadata, such as IP addresses, to serve and protect pages under its policies. Review GitHub's privacy statement if needed. We do not have a custom analytics tracker on the launch edition.

Limitations

Revision Ledger is for documenting requests and estimating potential extra work. Its values are not accounting, tax, legal, invoice, or contract-enforcement advice. You are responsible for confirming approvals and terms with your customer.

10-Ball Ghost Journal is a manual training diary; it does not verify performance or offer professional coaching.

Feedback

To ask about these tools or report a problem, email doihaidang@gmail.com. If you voluntarily email us, your email provider and ours handle your message under their respective policies. We use your correspondence only to respond to your inquiry, unless you separately agree otherwise.

Pricing

These are free launch editions. No payment system is currently connected. Future premium features or prices, if any, will be presented clearly before purchase.
