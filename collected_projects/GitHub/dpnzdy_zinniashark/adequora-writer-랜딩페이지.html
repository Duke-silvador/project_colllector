WHY

Why a local writing tool, now

We're entrusting more and more of our records to the cloud. It's convenient — but it costs us something.

When a service shuts down or changes its policy, your records can vanish with it.

Your writing and photos sit on someone else's server, handled in ways you can't fully see.

Some records stay accessible only as long as you keep paying, and without an internet connection, you often can't open anything at all.

"What would it take for a record to still open, in my own hands, ten years from now?"

The answer was simple: not the cloud, not an account, not any company's server — just a single file on your own computer.

VALUES

Core values

Six principles shape ZinniaShark Note. Each code names one condition every document has to meet.

OWN — Ownership

Full local ownership

Documents are stored directly on your computer, not somewhere online. No sign-up, no login, no upload to a server.

PKG — Package

One file, every kind of record

Text, tables, images, audio, and video live together in a single .adq file, instead of scattered across separate apps.

SEC — Security

Strong encryption

When password protection is enabled, your document and embedded media are secured using Argon2id key derivation and AES-256-GCM authenticated encryption.

EDIT — Editing

Fast editing, nothing extra

Headings, lists, quotes, code, tables, links. A keyboard-first flow that keeps pace with your thinking.

MAP — Structure

Structure you never get lost in

An outline view shows the shape of a long document at a glance, and jumps you straight to any point in it.

SAVE — Recovery

Steady, even when things go wrong

Autosave and recovery files mean a sudden crash never costs you your work.

MECHANISM

Trust, proven by engineering

"Secure" isn't a promise here — it's a structure. The document format is publicly documented as adq-spec, and it adopts only internationally vetted cryptographic standards.

ADQ1 CONTAINER

No HTML or scripting language is used as the document format — so a document can never carry executable code inside it.
