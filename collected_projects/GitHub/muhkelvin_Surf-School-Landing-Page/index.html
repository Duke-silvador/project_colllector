The Kala Philosophy

Why Learn With Us

Many tourist surf schools rush thirty beginners into heavy reform waves at the same time. We prioritize small coach ratios, thoughtful tide-reading, and calm encouragement so you feel completely secure from your first step in the water.

Small-Ratio Water Attention

You will never be left alone in a pack. Your instructor stays beside your board to steady you against swell and guide your take-off.

Tide & Sandbar Timing

We plan lesson times strictly around favorable incoming mid-tides and soft sandy bottoms—never shallow low-tide coral hazards.

All Gear Included Upfront

From soft high-buoyancy foam boards to UV long-sleeve rashguards and mineral zinc, everything is prepared when you arrive.

Patient Lifeguard-Certified Coaches

All instructors hold certified coastal lifesaving qualifications and communicate warmly in fluent English and Indonesian.

Clear Answers Before You Paddle

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our lessons, equipment, ocean safety, and scheduling.

Do I need previous surfing or swimming experience?

+

No prior surfing experience is required. For beginner lessons, we practice in waist-deep water on a gentle sandy bottom.

Basic swimming comfort (ability to tread water and not panic in knee-to-chest water) is helpful, and your instructor remains within arm's reach of your surfboard at all times.

What should I bring to the beach lesson?

+

Just bring swimwear (boardshorts or swimsuit to wear underneath our rashguard), a beach towel, and dry clothes to change into afterward.

We provide long-sleeve UV rashguards, premium mineral zinc sunscreen, high-volume soft-top surfboards, and secure storage for your backpack and valuables at our beach base.

Is the surfboard and rashguard included in the price?

+

Yes, 100% of the necessary gear is included in every lesson package. There are no surprise rental fees on the sand.

How long does each surf session last?

+

Beginner (First Wave) and Private sessions run for 90 minutes (20 minutes of beach theory/warmup + 70 minutes of coaching in the water). Group sessions run for 120 minutes with extended water intervals and pauses to catch your breath.

Can I book a dedicated 1-on-1 private lesson?

+

Absolutely. Our Private Surf Lesson (IDR 650.000) guarantees an exclusive instructor dedicated entirely to your waves, offering the fastest learning curve and customized pacing.

What happens if the ocean conditions or weather are unsuitable?

+

Safety is non-negotiable. If heavy storms, unfavorable currents, or unsafe swell size occur on your booked day, we immediately notify you via WhatsApp to reschedule to the next safe tide window or offer a full free cancellation.

How do I confirm my booking and schedule?

+

Simply message us via WhatsApp with your target date. We check the tide chart, suggest the ideal 90-minute time slot (usually between 7:30 AM and 11:00 AM or late afternoon), and send our beach location pin.

Can children or teenagers join a surf lesson?

+

Yes! Children aged 7 and above can join. For children under 12, we require a 1-on-1 private lesson ratio so an instructor stays strictly with the young surfer throughout the entire water session.
