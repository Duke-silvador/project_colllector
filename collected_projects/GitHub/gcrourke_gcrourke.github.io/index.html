Gabriel Rourke

PhD Candidate, Economics

Boston University

I am an PhD candidate in Economics at Boston University. My research is in labor economics, health economics, and industrial organization with a focus on antitrust and labor market outcomes.

I served as the co-chair of the BU Graduate Economics Association 2025-2026. Before studying at Boston University, I worked as an RA at the London School of Economics and as a research analysis for RPB Energy Economics. I studied my undergrad in mathematics and economics at the University of Chicago and my Master's degree in Economics from BU.
