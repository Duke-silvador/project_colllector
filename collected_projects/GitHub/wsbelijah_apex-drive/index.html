Questions

How old do I need to be to rent?

21 or older with a valid driver's license for most of the lineup. Exotics (the Huracán and the 720S) are 25+. A young-driver fee applies under 25 on Sport and Luxury cars.

How does the security deposit work?

It's a refundable hold on your card at pickup, released when the car comes back as it left. Electric cars start at $500, Sport and Luxury at $1,000, Exotics at $2,500. You're told the exact amount before you book — never after.

What about insurance?

Your own full-coverage policy usually transfers to a rental — bring proof. If it doesn't, daily coverage can be added at booking. Every rental includes roadside assistance either way.

Can you deliver the car to me?

Yes — anywhere in the LA area between 8 AM and 10 PM, $49 flat. Choose "Delivered to me" in the booking panel and we'll confirm the address by text.
