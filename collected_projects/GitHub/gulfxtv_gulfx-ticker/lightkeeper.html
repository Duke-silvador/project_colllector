Lightkeeper of the Week

Every week, we shine the light on someone who lights the way for others on the Mississippi Gulf Coast.

Long before GPS, sailors counted on one person to make it home safe — the lighthouse keeper. Night after night, through storms and darkness, keepers climbed those stairs and kept the lamp burning. Not for recognition. So others could find their way.

Our Coast is full of lightkeepers: the neighbor who checks on the elderly couple down the street, the coach who buys cleats for the kid who can’t afford them, the volunteer who shows up every single time. Each week, GulfX TV turns the light around and shines it on one of them.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can be nominated?

Anyone on the Mississippi Gulf Coast whose kindness, service, or generosity makes a difference — neighbors, volunteers, teachers, coaches, first responders, and quiet givers of every kind.

Can I nominate myself or a family member?

We love the enthusiasm, but nominations should come from someone else — a neighbor, friend, coworker, or community member who has seen the light firsthand.

How is each week's Lightkeeper chosen?

Our team reviews every nomination and selects one honoree each week based on the impact of their story and the difference they make in the community.

What does the honoree receive?

Each Lightkeeper of the Week is featured by GulfX TV with their story shared across our platforms — and every weekly honoree becomes a finalist for Lightkeeper of the Year.

Every Honoree Becomes a Finalist

Each Lightkeeper of the Week automatically becomes a finalist for Lightkeeper of the Year — crowned Friday, November 5th, 2027.
