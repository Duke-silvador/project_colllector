Every week, GulfX TV honors a Lightkeeper of the Week — a Coast neighbor whose kindness lights the way for others. Once a year, we bring all 52 weekly honorees together for one unforgettable evening of red carpet, dinner, and celebration — and crown one of them Lightkeeper of the Year.

It's the finale the weekly segment builds toward all year: every story told, every life touched, every light kept burning — honored together in one room.

How It Works

  • Every Lightkeeper of the Week honoree automatically becomes a finalist for Lightkeeper of the Year.
  • Finalists' stories are re-shared across GulfX TV platforms in the weeks before the event.
  • The community votes for their Lightkeeper of the Year, combined with a judging panel's scores.
  • The winner is revealed live on awards night — sealed envelope, on stage, in front of every honoree.

The Night

Red-carpet arrivals. Dinner. Each finalist's story on the big screen. And one crowning moment as the Coast's brightest light takes home the title of Lightkeeper of the Year — with an on-air feature, a commemorative award, and a place in GulfX TV history.

The inaugural Lightkeeper of the Year celebration takes place Friday, November 5th, 2027 at 7pm — following a full year of weekly honorees. Email info@gulfxtv.com for updates.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does someone become a finalist?

By being honored as a Lightkeeper of the Week. Every weekly honoree is automatically a finalist — no separate nomination needed.

Who picks the winner?

A combination of community voting and a judging panel, revealed live on awards night.

Can I attend?

Yes — ticket details will be announced closer to the event. Weekly honorees and their families are our guests of honor.

When is the first one?

Friday, November 5th, 2027 — after a full year of weekly Lightkeepers.
