How Winners Are Chosen

  • 1. Nominations logged — every nomination is recorded; multiples for one business combine into a single stronger entry.
  • 2. Eligibility screening — ineligible entries are removed.
  • 3. Panel scoring — 3–5 judges score each nomination 1–10 on community impact (30%), customer experience (25%), giveback (25%), and nomination strength (20%). Top 3–5 per category become finalists.
  • 4. Finalists announced (March) — finalists go public; ticket sales open.
  • 5. Public voting (March) — one vote per person per category; 50% of the final score.
  • 6. Final tally — panel scores (50%) plus public vote (50%), double-checked and sealed.
  • 7. Awards night (April 30th) — sealed envelopes, live on-stage reveal.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I nominate my own business?

Yes. Self-nominations are welcome — just tell us what makes your business a beacon.

Which businesses are eligible?

Any business physically located in Hancock, Harrison, Jackson, Pearl River, Stone, or George counties. Brightest Newcomer must be under 3 years old; Legacy Beacon must be 25+ years.

Can one business win multiple categories?

A business can be nominated in multiple categories but can only take home one award per year — except People’s Choice, which can stack.

Can a past winner win again?

Not in the same category two years running, but they can be nominated in a different category.

How are winners chosen?

Finalists are picked by a judging panel scoring nominations on community impact, customer experience, giveback, and nomination strength. Winners are 50% panel score + 50% public vote. Sealed until awards night.

Where does the money go?

Proceeds help fund GulfX TV’s community programming and support local organizations backing causes across the Mississippi Gulf Coast.
