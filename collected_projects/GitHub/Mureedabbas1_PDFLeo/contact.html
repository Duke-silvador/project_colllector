How can we help?

For help with a PDFLeo tool, account access, a technical issue, or a privacy question, contact our support email. Please describe what happened and which tool or page you were using.

Email hello@pdfleo.online

To help us investigate

  • Include the tool name and the steps that led to the problem.
  • Tell us which browser and device you are using.
  • Include the exact error message, if there is one.
  • Please do not email passwords, payment details, or confidential documents.
Document privacy: If you report a PDF-processing issue, describe it without attaching sensitive files. Browser-based tools are designed to process files locally where indicated; check the Privacy Policy for details and limitations.
