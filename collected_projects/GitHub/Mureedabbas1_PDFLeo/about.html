About PDFLeo

PDF tasks, made simpler.

PDFLeo is a lightweight website for common PDF tasks such as merging, splitting, rotating, extracting text and converting images to PDF. The goal is to make everyday document work straightforward, accessible and transparent.

Many tools process files directly in your browser. Some advanced features are still limited or need specialist processing services, and the site labels these limitations rather than promising results it cannot provide.

Our approach

  • Clarity: tool descriptions explain what the current version can and cannot do.
  • Privacy-conscious design: browser-based tools are designed to process files on your device, while the site may still load third-party libraries and record basic tool usage.
  • Continuous improvement: reliability, accessible design, useful guides and better conversions are priorities.

PDFLeo is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by iLovePDF or other PDF services.
