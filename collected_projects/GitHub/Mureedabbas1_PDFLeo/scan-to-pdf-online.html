Create a PDF from Scanned Pages

A scan-to-PDF workflow is useful for receipts, paper forms, notes, and signed pages. Good capture quality and page ordering are essential. A scan may remain image-only until OCR creates a searchable text layer.

Open Scan to PDF Tool

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Open PDFLeo and choose Scan to PDF.
  2. Select the PDF or supported document from your device.
  3. Use the options shown by the tool and check the selected pages or settings.
  4. Run the operation and download the output if processing completes.
  5. Open the downloaded file and confirm the result before sharing or relying on it.

Tips for a better result

  • Use even lighting and avoid shadows.
  • Keep every page edge in frame.
  • Use OCR when searchable text is required and proofread its output.
Important: Keep an original copy of important documents. Always review the exported file; results can vary with file structure, quality, and supported features.

Frequently asked questions

Is a scanned PDF automatically searchable?

Not always; OCR may be needed.

Can I combine several pages?

Use multi-file selection and ordering controls if available, then verify the page count.
