Solana Token Safety Checklist: Don't Get Bitten

Before touching the swap button, run the checklist. If a critical answer is missing, the token stays outside the bunker.

ZOMBIECASH SURVIVOR ARCHIVES · Updated 24 October 2026 · Educational information, not financial advice.

FIELD RULE: This guide helps you investigate risks; it cannot certify a token as safe or guarantee an outcome. Never share a seed phrase or private key.

Identity — same mint, every time

☐ Copy the mint address from an official source. ☐ Compare the full address in your explorer and trading interface. ☐ Ignore lookalike tickers, logos and sponsored search results. Verify on Solana Explorer.

Liquidity — can you exit?

☐ Check pool liquidity and pool age on DEX Screener. ☐ Compare trade size with available liquidity and expected slippage. ☐ Check whether liquidity is concentrated in one pool or can change abruptly. High volume does not guarantee an easy exit.

Holders — who owns the horde?

☐ Inspect top holders. ☐ Separate known pool/burn addresses from ordinary wallets where possible. ☐ Look for clusters of wallets with suspiciously similar balances or coordinated activity. Concentration raises risk; it does not independently prove wrongdoing.

Authorities and token features

☐ Review mint/freeze authority and token extensions using current, reputable tooling. ☐ Understand what each permission can do for this token standard. ☐ Treat unexplained control, recent metadata changes or unusual restrictions as reasons to pause.

Trading quality

☐ Compare buys, sells, transaction count and volume across more than one short interval. ☐ Watch for wash-like patterns, sudden liquidity drops and price moves on tiny trades. ☐ Do not mistake a bot signal or trending rank for an endorsement.

Wallet and website

☐ Check the exact domain and certificate; avoid links in unsolicited DMs. ☐ Never share seed phrases or private keys. ☐ Read every wallet prompt and reject unexpected permissions or transactions. ☐ Consider using a separate wallet with only the funds needed.

Final go / no-go

If the mint cannot be verified, the site is a clone, the pool is too thin, the risk is unclear or anyone pressures you to hurry: NO TRADE. Zombies can wait. Your wallet should too.
