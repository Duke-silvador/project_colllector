One ship. One commander. Eighteen star systems.

Cosmic Commando is a top-down space combat game set in a scaled, real solar system and the stars around it. Take missions from orbital stations, answer Mayday calls from embattled fleets, and fight alien fighters, drone swarms and destroyers from Earth orbit to the alien-held stars beyond the jump gates.

Features

Real star systems

Fly from Earth orbit to Alpha Centauri, Tau Ceti and TRAPPIST-1. Eight human systems are linked by jump gates; ten alien systems wait further out.

Fleet battles

Answer a Mayday and fight beside carriers, battleships and destroyers while thirty allied fighters launch around you.

Missions from stations

Station defence, escorts, convoys, rescues and bounty hunts on a named alien Overseer. Dock, take the job, launch.

Upgrades and overclocks

Spend credits on ten tiers of shields, hull, engines and weapons. Collect alien tech for short, stacking overclocks mid-fight.

A story told over comms

Station commanders, admirals and captains call you by name. Pick your pilot from twenty faces and write your own commander.

Arcade or Newtonian

Snappy arcade flight with strafing by default, or classic thrust-and-drift for purists. Full keyboard, mouse and gamepad support.
