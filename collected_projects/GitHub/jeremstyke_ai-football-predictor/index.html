How it is calculated

A method well known to football statisticians, applied every day to every match.

01Team strength

Attack and defence of each team from its recent matches, with home advantage.

02Expected goals

That gives how many goals each team should score in this match.

03Every score

A Poisson model gives the probability of every score, then win, draw and loss.

04Locked, checked

The probability is frozen before the match, then compared with the real result.

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes. The website, the web app, the Telegram mini app and the widget are free, with no sign-up.

Is this betting advice?

No. These are statistical estimates for information only, with no guaranteed result. 18+ only. If gambling is a problem for you, get help from a support line in your country.

Why doesn't the most likely result always happen?

Something at 60% fails 4 times out of 10, and that is expected. A model is judged over hundreds of matches, which is why the history is public.

How do I install the app?

On iPhone, open the site in Safari, tap Share then “Add to Home Screen”. On Android, browser menu then “Install app”. Or open the mini app in Telegram.

Where does the data come from?

Fixtures and results come from a sports data provider. The probabilities, expected goals, simulated tables and previews come from our own model.
