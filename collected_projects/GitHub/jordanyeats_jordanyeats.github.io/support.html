Support

Buyout is a strategy board game of founding, investing, and merging — played entirely against AI rivals, entirely on your device.

Learning the game

Tap Learn the game on the main screen for a seven-lesson interactive tutorial covering placement, founding, buying, majorities, mergers, settlement, and the closing bell.

Common questions

Where are my saves? On your device. An unfinished game resumes from the main screen; statistics and honors live under The Record.

Does the game need an internet connection? No. Buyout is fully offline.

Sound isn't playing? Check the Sound toggle on the main screen and your device's silent switch.

Contact

Bugs, questions, or ideas: open an issue on the Buyout tracker. Please include your app version (shown at the bottom of the main screen) and your device model.
