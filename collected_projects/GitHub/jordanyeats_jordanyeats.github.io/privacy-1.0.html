Privacy Policy

Superseded. This policy applied to Buyout 1.0, which had no advertising. The current policy is at privacy.html.

Buyout stores everything on your device, and nothing anywhere else.

What the app stores

Game saves, play statistics, honors, sound preferences, and any player names you type. All of it lives in the app's local storage on your phone or tablet. None of it is transmitted.

What the app collects

Nothing. Buyout has no accounts, no login, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party data services. The game makes no network requests and is fully playable offline.

Data sharing

There is nothing to share. No data leaves your device.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes all of its data. There is no copy anywhere else.

Children

Buyout is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If a future version of Buyout ever changes any of the above, this policy will be updated before that version ships.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on the Buyout tracker.

Buyout collects nothing and stores nothing off your device, so there is no personal data held about you to request, correct, or delete.
