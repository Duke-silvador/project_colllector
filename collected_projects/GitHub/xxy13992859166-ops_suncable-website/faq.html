Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common questions about our PV cables, certifications, and manufacturing.

What PV cable standards do you manufacture?

We manufacture PV1-F (IEC 62930), H1ZZ2-K (VDE 0250-214), and UL 4703 / CSA C22.2 No. 214 certified solar DC cables. Our cables meet TUV, UL, CE, and CSA standards for global market access. Standard cross-sections range from 2.5mm² to 6mm², with custom sizes available.

What is the typical lead time for orders?

Standard orders ship within 7-15 working days. Custom color, size, or packaging orders require 15-25 working days. Rush orders are available upon request. We maintain large inventory of common specifications for faster delivery.

What is your MOQ (Minimum Order Quantity)?

Our MOQ starts from 1,000 meters per specification. We offer flexible ordering for sample orders and small project requirements. Contact us for specific MOQ details based on your cable type and quantity.

Do you offer free samples?

Yes, we provide free samples up to 50 meters per cable type. Customers only need to cover shipping costs. Sample delivery takes 3-7 days via DHL/FedEx. We can also provide video inspections of production and testing.

What certifications do your PV cables hold?

Our cables carry TUV Rheinland, UL Listed, CSA, and CE certifications. All products comply with IEC 62930 and VDE 0250-214 standards. We can provide certification documents and test reports for customs clearance and project approval.

Can you customize cable colors and markings?

Yes, we support custom outer jacket colors (black, red, blue, orange, green, yellow, etc.), custom length markings, and branded packaging. Minimum customization order may apply. Please contact our sales team for details.

What is the service life of your solar cables?

Our XLPO insulated solar cables have a designed service life of over 25 years under normal operating conditions (temperature -40°C to +120°C). They are UV resistant, ozone resistant, and suitable for outdoor rooftop and ground-mounted solar installations.

Where is your factory located?

Our manufacturing facility is located in Xi'an, Shaanxi Province, China. We have over 20 years of experience in PV cable production. Factory visits and video tours are welcome. Contact us to arrange a visit.
