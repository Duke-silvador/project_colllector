Typical Applications

PV1-F solar cable is the most widely used cable for photovoltaic array interconnections. It connects solar panels to each other, to combiner boxes, and to inverters. The red/black dual-core design simplifies installation by providing both positive and negative conductors in one cable.

  • Ground-mount solar power plants
  • Rooftop commercial and residential solar installations
  • Solar carport and agrivoltaic projects
  • PV module to inverter string connections
