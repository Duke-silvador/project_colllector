Our Product Range for Indian Projects

PV1-F Solar Cable

Red/black dual-core cable (0.6/1kV) for PV array interconnections. TUV certified, XLPO insulation, -40 to +120°C operating temperature. Most popular choice for African and Middle East solar projects.

H1Z2Z2-K Solar Cable

Single-core black cable (1kV) for DC string connections. IEC 62930 compliant, UV resistant, suitable for rooftop and ground mount installations across hot climates.

MC4 Solar Connectors

IP68 rated MC4-Evo2 connector sets (male/female pair). 1500V DC rated, tool-free snap-lock design. Essential for PV module to cable connections.

Storage DC Cable

Low-smoke halogen-free DC cable for energy storage battery banks. Optimized for residential and commercial solar + storage systems.
