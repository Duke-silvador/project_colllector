How to Import Solar Cables from China

Step-by-step guide for importing TUV & UL certified solar cables from China.

Step 1: Send Inquiry

Tell us your cable specifications, quantities, and delivery destination. We respond within 2 hours with a formal quotation including FOB/CIF prices.

Step 2: Confirm Order

Review the quotation, confirm specifications, and sign the sales contract. We accept T/T (30% deposit) and L/C at sight.

Step 3: Production & QC

Orders produced within 7-15 days. We provide production photos and pre-shipment inspection reports for your verification.

Step 4: Shipping & Documentation

We handle export documentation: Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Certificate of Origin, Bill of Lading, and test reports.

Step 5: Customs Clearance

Our documentation meets all international customs requirements. We provide HS codes and country-specific compliance certificates.

Step 6: After-Sales Support

25-year warranty on all cables. Dedicated account manager for any quality concerns or reordering.
