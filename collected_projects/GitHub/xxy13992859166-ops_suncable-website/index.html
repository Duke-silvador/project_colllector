FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our solar PV cables and ordering process.

Q: What certifications do your solar cables have?

All our solar cables are TUV certified (Germany), UL listed (USA), CE marked (Europe), and comply with IEC 62930 and VDE standards. We hold ISO 9001 certification for our manufacturing process.

Q: What is the difference between PV1-F and H1Z2Z2-K cables?

PV1-F is a dual-core cable (red/black) used for PV module output connections. H1Z2Z2-K is a single-core cable used for DC string connections between modules. Both use XLPO insulation rated for -40°C to +120°C.

Q: What is your minimum order quantity?

Our MOQ is 1km per color and specification. We support flexible ordering for both small distributors and large utility-scale EPC projects.

Q: How long is the delivery time?

Standard orders ship within 7-15 days. Custom specifications may take 15-20 days. We ship worldwide via sea freight and air freight to 50+ countries.
