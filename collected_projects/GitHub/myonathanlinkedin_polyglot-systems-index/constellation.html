🌌 Visitor Guide • 3D Cosmic Knowledge Universe

What Is This? Welcome to the 3D Cosmic Knowledge Universe of Mateus Yonathan. This interactive galaxy maps our entire public portfolio—517 open-source software modules across 14 programming languages and 201 published Research Papers—as interconnected stars in space.

What Do the Stars & Galaxies Mean?
• 🌟 Radiant 4-Ray Supernovas: Published Research Papers & Mini-Papers with executive summaries and full PDFs.
• 🔵 Glowing Star Spheres: Open-source code modules across 14 language ecosystems (Python, Rust, C++, Go, Zig, TypeScript, etc.).
• 🪐 5 Galaxy Clusters & Constellation Lines: Group related works by scientific domain and connect papers to their companion software modules.

How to Explore (Visitor Controls):
• Click Any Star: Opens the portfolio summary card on the right with direct buttons to read the PDF or view the GitHub repository.
• Search & Filter: Use the left panel or press Ctrl+K to search any topic and fly directly to that star.
• Camera & Panels: Drag to orbit, scroll/pinch to zoom, or use ☰ Left Panel and 📋 Right Panel in the top bar to show/hide panels anytime.
