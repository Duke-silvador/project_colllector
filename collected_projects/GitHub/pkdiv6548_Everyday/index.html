SMALL THINGS, BIG EXPLANATIONS

Everyday mysteries, decoded.

The next time something feels ordinary, pause for a second. There may be a surprising explanation hiding underneath.

01 / FOOD✳

How does a popcorn kernel explode into a fluffy snack?

A little water inside the kernel becomes steam. Pressure builds until the shell breaks and hot starch expands.

Unpack the science ↗02 / FOOD◉

Why does a cut banana turn brown so quickly?

When fruit cells are cut, enzymes meet oxygen in the air and start a colour-changing reaction. Lemon juice can slow it down.

Unpack the science ↗03 / PHYSICS↗

Can a balloon pull paper without touching it?

Rubbing transfers electric charge. The charged balloon can attract tiny paper pieces through an invisible electric force.

YOUR MINI FIELD GUIDE

Science is not just something to read.

Make a prediction, change one thing, and notice what happens. These low-cost activities use everyday objects. Start with a grown-up if you're a child, and never experiment with electricity, sealed containers, dangerous heat or unknown chemicals.
