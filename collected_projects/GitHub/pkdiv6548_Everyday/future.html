A PRACTICAL START

Your first 30 days.

Week 1 — Understand your goalChoose one problem or field you care about. Read two reliable introductions and write down what you want to understand.
Week 2 — Learn the foundationsStudy one beginner course or guide. Keep notes of new terms and check them against trusted documentation.
Week 3 — Build something smallCreate a tiny project, case study, experiment or portfolio item that demonstrates what you learned.
Week 4 — Review and choose the next stepAsk for feedback, compare your work with real requirements and decide whether to continue, change direction or deepen a skill.
Important: forecasts are not guarantees. Technology adoption and job demand vary by region and change over time. Before investing money in a course or changing careers, compare current job listings, official labour-market information, course costs and your own circumstances. This page is educational, not a promise of employment or income.
