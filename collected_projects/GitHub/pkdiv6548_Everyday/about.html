WHY WE LOOK CLOSER

The extraordinary is hiding in plain sight.

Everyday Oddities is a curious corner of the internet for people who like asking one more question. We explore the science behind ordinary moments—with clarity, warmth and a sense of wonder.

✳

Curiosity for everyone

You don't need a lab coat or a science degree to enjoy science. You only need to notice, wonder, and stay open to evidence. Our English/Hindi switch helps more readers explore in the language they prefer.

◉

Wonder with responsibility

Science writing should be clear about what is known and what is simplified. Our short explainers are starting points, not a substitute for textbooks, specialist advice or primary research. Always test claims against trustworthy sources.

↗

Built to be explored

Browse by topic, search for a question, or try a tiny experiment. The site is designed to work across phones, tablets and desktop screens without a build step.

♡

Keep asking

The best discovery often begins with three simple words: “Why does that happen?” We hope this site gives you a few more reasons to ask.
