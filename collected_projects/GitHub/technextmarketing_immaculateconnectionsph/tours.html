Good to know

Tour package questions, answered

What tour packages does Immaculate Connections offer?
We currently offer 14 Vietnam packages from Manila: 8 Da Nang tours and 6 Hanoi, Sapa and Ha Long Bay tours. Every package page lists the itinerary, inclusions, exclusions and travel dates, and we also build custom itineraries for families, companies and groups.
How much do the tour packages cost?
Rates start at From ₱22,888 per pax and differ by departure date; each package page shows the base rate and the amount added on dearer dates. Custom trips are quoted on request, and every quotation is free.
Where do the tours depart from?
International packages depart from Manila (NAIA). Custom trips around Cebu pick up from Cebu City or Mactan hotels.
Do you offer day tours from Cebu?
Yes. Send an inquiry with your date and group size and we will quote a day tour around Cebu.
How do I book a tour package?
Open the package page and click “Request a quote”, or use the inquiry form and choose your package. We confirm availability for your dates and send a quotation with the payment details. Your booking is confirmed once payment is received.
