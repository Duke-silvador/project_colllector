Service 04

Vietnam Tour Packages & Custom Trips

Fixed-departure tours to Da Nang, Hanoi, Sapa and Ha Long Bay from Manila, and custom trips around Cebu, Bohol, the Camotes Islands and beyond. Every package lists its departure dates, inclusions and the places you will visit.

  • Da Nang: 4D3N to 6D4N tours by charter or scheduled flight, with Ba Na Hills, the Golden Bridge and Hoi An, some with a night at Ba Na Hills.
  • Hanoi, Sapa & Ha Long Bay: 4D3N to 6D4N programmes with the Fansipan cable car, the Old Quarter and a Ha Long Bay cruise, including No Shopping options.
  • Custom trips: Cebu, Bohol, Camotes, Boracay, El Nido and Coron itineraries built around your dates and group.
  • Departures: fixed dates from November 2026 to July 2027, all from Manila, with the rate shown for each date.
