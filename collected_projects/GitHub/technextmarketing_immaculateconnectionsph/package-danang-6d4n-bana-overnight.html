Know before you go

Your Vietnam travel guide

Practical tips for Vietnam, from entry rules to what to eat. General guidance for a Philippine traveller — rules and prices change, so confirm the latest before you fly. Need a hand with the visa or tickets? Our team is glad to help.

Entry requirements

Visa-free up to 21 days

Philippine passport holders enter Vietnam visa-free for stays up to 21 days. Your passport must be valid at least 6 months beyond your return date and have blank pages. No vaccinations are required; just keep routine shots up to date.

Money

Vietnamese dong (₫)

Cash rules for markets, taxis and street food; cards work in hotels, malls and larger restaurants. Tipping is not expected, but rounding up or ₫20–50k for guides and drivers is appreciated. Budget roughly ₱1,500–2,500 a day beyond your package for meals and extras.

Weather & what to pack

Warm and humid

Central Vietnam (Da Nang, Hoi An) is hot most of the year; the north (Hanoi, Sapa) is cooler and can be chilly from December to February. Pack light breathable clothes, a light jacket for the north, a rain layer, sunblock and comfy walking shoes.

Getting around

Grab is your friend

Use the Grab app for cars and motorbike taxis at fair, up-front prices. Cities are walkable, and your package includes airport transfers and a private coach on tour days. Avoid unmetered street taxis.

Language

Vietnamese; some English

English is understood in hotels and tourist spots but limited elsewhere. A few words go far: Xin chào (hello), Cảm ơn (thank you), Bao nhiêu? (how much?). Your English-speaking guide handles the rest.

Culture & etiquette

Dress modestly at temples

Cover shoulders and knees at pagodas and remove your shoes when asked. Ask before photographing people, keep your voice down inside temples, and use both hands when giving or receiving. Friendly bargaining at markets is normal.

Safety & health

Safe; mind the traffic

Vietnam is safe for tourists; the main hazard is crossing busy roads, so walk slowly and steadily. Watch for bag-snatching in crowds and stick to Grab or hotel taxis. Drink bottled water. Emergencies: police 113, ambulance 115.

Connectivity

Cheap local SIM or eSIM

Grab a Viettel or Mobifone tourist SIM/eSIM at the airport for fast, affordable data. Wi-Fi is widespread in hotels and cafés. Handy apps: Grab, Google Maps and Google Translate with Vietnamese saved offline.

Must-see sights & food

Golden Bridge & mì Quảng

Do not miss Ba Na Hills’ Golden Bridge, Hoi An’s lantern-lit old town and the Marble Mountains. Eat your way through phở, bánh mì, mì Quảng, fresh spring rolls and Vietnamese egg coffee.
