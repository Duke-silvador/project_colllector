Risk disclosure & privacy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

1. Research, not advice

IntelRadar Research publishes the output of rules-based research engines: setups, levels and their measured outcomes. This content is general and impersonal. It is not investment, financial, legal or tax advice, and it does not take your situation, objectives or risk tolerance into account. Any decision to trade is yours alone.

2. Crypto is high risk

Crypto assets are highly volatile and largely unregulated. Prices can fall sharply and quickly, and you can lose all of the money you put in. Trading perpetual futures adds further risk, including liquidation. We never suggest leverage. Only trade money you can afford to lose.

3. No guarantees

Levels on a setup are a plan with a defined exit, not a promise. Many setups are stopped out. Results shown on this site or in our channels, including target-reached cards and base rates, describe the past and do not predict future results. Measurements use exchange market data and may differ from the price you could actually trade at.

4. No offer or solicitation

Nothing published by IntelRadar Research is an offer or a solicitation to buy or sell any asset, and it is not directed at anyone in a jurisdiction where such content would be unlawful.

5. Independence

Coins are selected by the engines' scans of the market, not by hand. We do not accept payment to feature, list or promote any coin or project.

6. Privacy

This website sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from other companies' servers. It has no forms and collects no personal data. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses to operate and secure its service, as described in GitHub's own privacy statement.

Links to Telegram and X take you to those services, which have their own terms and privacy policies.
