Methodology

Every engine earns its place.

An engine reaches production only after it passes the same tests. These are the standards every one of them is held to, before release and after.

01

Rules before results

Each test is specified before it is run, so the outcome is measured, never tuned to fit.

02

Out-of-sample validation

An engine must hold on data it has never seen, walked forward through time rather than fitted to the past.

03

Costs included

Exchange fees and slippage are part of every test, so results reflect what is tradable.

04

No lookahead

Signals use closed candles only. No calculation can use a price that did not exist yet.

05

Statistical discipline

No claim is made before 30 trades. Our live forward test on an exchange demo account is assessed only after 60.

06

Measured in the open

Every published setup is tracked from the published price to its outcome, with targets and stops recorded alike.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What is IntelRadar Research?

A quantitative research desk for crypto markets. Rules-based engines scan 390 coins on Binance after every 4-hour close, publish complete setups with entry, invalidation and targets, and report every setup through to its outcome.

Is this investment advice?

No. IntelRadar Research publishes research. Setups are a plan with a defined exit, not a recommendation for your situation. Crypto assets are volatile and you can lose all of the money you put in; please read the risk disclosure.

How are results measured?

From the published price, on hourly data, and only while the setup's stop had not been hit. Target reports are shown with their base rate across all setups of the last 30 days, and outcome reports include stops and time exits.

Where does the data come from?

Binance spot and USD-M futures market data. Every signal is computed from closed candles only.

When does the Pro Desk open?

When @intelradarai reaches 2,000 followers on X. Follow the account to be first in. Until then, the Free tier on Telegram carries a daily sample of the desk's research.

How can I share feedback or work with you?

Message or reply to @intelradarai on X. Feature requests from the community shape the roadmap.
