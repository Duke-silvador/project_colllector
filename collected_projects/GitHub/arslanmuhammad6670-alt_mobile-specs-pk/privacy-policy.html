Privacy Policy

Check Specs is a static information website. We keep data collection to the minimum a specifications guide needs.

What we do not collect

There are no user accounts, no comments and no checkout on this site. We do not ask for or store your name, email address, phone number or payment details.

What is stored on your device

The currency selector saves your chosen currency in your browser's local storage so prices stay in your currency on your next visit. This never leaves your device and you can clear it any time from your browser settings.

Hosting and analytics

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security and operation, under GitHub's own privacy statement. We may use privacy-respecting, aggregate analytics (such as Google Analytics) to understand which devices and pages are popular; such analytics work on aggregated statistics, not on identifying individual visitors. Advertising partners, when present, may use cookies as described in their own policies; you can block cookies in your browser without losing access to any specification or price on this site.

External links

Pages may link to manufacturer and reference websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external sites.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page.
