Data Sources & Verification Policy

Specifications

Specification tables are compiled from the manufacturer's official product pages and GSMArena's full-specifications database. Each model's data is read from its own specification page — never copied from a similar model — and a second check is made when a model's details are updated after launch (for example, when rumoured figures are replaced by final ones).

Prices

Pakistani market prices are checked against current local listings, principally PriceOye's Pakistan price list. Where no Pakistani listing exists, the manufacturer's announced price in USD or EUR is converted at the mid-market rates referenced in the site footer and should be read as an estimate. Currency figures in the selector are conversions of the PKR price at the rates stated on the site; they are guides, not local shop prices.

Status (Continued / Discontinued)

Production status follows the manufacturer's announcements first and reference databases second; when they disagree, the manufacturer's position is shown.

Images

Device images are the manufacturers' official product images as carried by the reference sources above, shown for identification. A colour gallery is only published when a verified photo of that exact colour exists.

Freshness

New launches are added daily by automated checks against the reference databases, and prices of current models are refreshed regularly. If you find stale information, please report it via Contact Us.
