Editorial Policy

Verification before publication

No specification is published from memory or from a single unverified listing. Every model's specification table is read from its official product page or GSMArena's full-specifications page and matched field by field before the model goes live. If a field cannot be verified, it is left out rather than guessed.

Sources in order of trust

1) The manufacturer's official product/support page. 2) GSMArena's specifications database. 3) Established retailer and reference listings, used mainly for prices. Where sources disagree on a status question — for example whether a model is discontinued — the manufacturer's position wins.

Prices

Pakistani prices come from current local market listings wherever a model is actually sold in Pakistan. Models not officially sold here are shown with an approximate converted price or "Price not announced". Rumoured or unannounced devices, when listed, are labelled with their expected status and their figures are replaced by final verified data at launch.

Corrections

Errors reported by readers are checked against the sources above and corrected as quickly as verification allows — see Contact Us. Silent edits are limited to obvious typos; factual corrections follow the verified source.

Independence

No brand, shop or advertiser can pay to change a specification, a price or a device's position on this site.
