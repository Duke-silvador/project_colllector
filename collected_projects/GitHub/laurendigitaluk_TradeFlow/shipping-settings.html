How shipping works

Simple manual handoff

1. Choose a service
Open one of your saved shipping provider links from the Buying request.
2. Create and pay for the shipment
You create the shipment directly with your chosen provider. TradeFlow does not collect or pay the shipping charge.
3. Upload the handoff
Return to TradeFlow, upload the label and QR code if provided, enter the tracking number and send the shipping instructions to the customer.
