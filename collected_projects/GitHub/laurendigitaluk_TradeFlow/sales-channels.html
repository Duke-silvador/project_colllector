Multi-channel rule

One item, multiple listings

TradeFlow keeps the physical Inventory asset as the master record. A listing belongs to that asset and one sales channel. If the item sells on one channel, the other active listings must be identified as DELIST REQUIRED so the physical item cannot remain available elsewhere.

Automatic cross-channel delisting will be added after the channel management layer and order/sale transition have been verified. No marketplace account is connected by this page.
