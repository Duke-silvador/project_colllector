Find products
Find the products you buy.
  1. Choose a Category, Branch and Manufacturer, or search for a specific model or product.
  2. Select only the model/package configurations you want in your Buying Catalogue.
  3. TradeFlow loads products on demand, so the full Master Catalogue is not downloaded.
  4. Once selected, the product is linked to its category, branch and manufacturer automatically.
  5. Choose how you want to value the product: Manual valuation, Manual price / override or Automatic pricing.
  6. Off removes the product from active Buying and clears its buying-price configuration.
  7. Research is kept separate and is read-only here. Available research can be used by your automatic pricing rules.
