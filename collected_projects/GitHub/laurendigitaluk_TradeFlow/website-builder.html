You no longer need to fill in lots of boxes. Choose a page, then click directly on the website preview to change its text or add an image.

Quick setup — four main steps
  1. Add your logo and business name.
  2. Choose your brand colours.
  3. Choose a template. The same layout is used in the live website.
  4. Save the draft, check Preview, then Publish.

Your What We Buy categories and What We Sell products are connected automatically. You do not rebuild product lists inside the website template.
