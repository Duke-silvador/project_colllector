Email

Your business email

Not set

Just enter the email address your business uses. TradeFlow will use it for important business notifications and as the reply address for customer emails.

You do not need to configure SMTP, mail servers or individual notification switches. TradeFlow handles the technical email setup for you.

Website identity

Website branding

Managed in Website Builder

Your customer-facing logo and website banner are now kept together in one place: Website Builder → Branding. This avoids having the same branding controls in two different areas.

Your business name and customer-facing business details remain here in Business Settings. Website Builder controls the logo, homepage banner, templates, colours, backgrounds, typography and page content.

Apple Pay

Apple Pay is a Stripe wallet rather than a separate Checkout API payment-method type. TradeFlow cannot independently switch Apple Pay on or off from this setting while card payments remain enabled. Its availability is controlled by Stripe and the customer's device/browser eligibility.

Card payments remain available and cannot be disabled here because TradeFlow uses card checkout as the base online payment method. Link, Klarna and Amazon Pay can be independently enabled or disabled below.
