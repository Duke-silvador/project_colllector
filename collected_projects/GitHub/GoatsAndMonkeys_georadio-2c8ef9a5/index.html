Prototype: Apple's free 30-second previews stand in for full songs. With MusicKit (an Apple Developer account) the same player plays whole tracks, explicit versions included. Songs are ranked on your phone from the places they rap about, anywhere in the US; the closest and best-known come first. Keep this page open; when the phone is locked it keeps playing and uses your last known location. Place data © OpenStreetMap contributors.
