HELPFUL INFORMATION

Bali Belly Recovery – Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about home visits, treatment packages and booking.

How many Bali Belly treatment packages are available?

There are 16 package options in two groups: B-Complex and Multivitamin. Options vary by additional components such as additional Electrolyte Solution (total 1,000 ml), fever reducer (antipyretic) and oral medication. The doctor will assess each patient and determine whether the listed components are clinically appropriate.

Are the prices shown promotional prices?

Displayed package prices reflect the promotion currently shown on this website and cover the services and components listed for the selected package. Any item or service outside the listed inclusions will be discussed with you before it is provided.

What is included in the service?

The service includes a doctor consultation and a doctor-and-nurse home visit, available 24 hours a day, every day, in our selected Bali service areas. A medical report may be provided to support an insurance claim. Treatment and medication depend on the doctor’s individual assessment and clinical judgement.

Do all packages include oral medication?

No. Oral medication is included only in selected packages and may include Anti-Diarrhea Medication, Anti-Nausea/Vomiting Medication, and Stomach Cramp Medication. Antibiotics are included only if medically indicated and prescribed by the doctor.

Can you visit my villa, hotel or holiday rental?

Doctor and nurse home visits are available 24 hours a day, every day, to villas, hotels and holiday rentals in our listed service areas. Contact our team to confirm your location and arrange the visit.

What if my symptoms are severe or getting worse?

Do not wait for a home visit if you have fainting, confusion, severe or worsening abdominal pain, blood in stool or vomit, or signs of serious dehydration. Seek urgent medical care at the nearest emergency department.
