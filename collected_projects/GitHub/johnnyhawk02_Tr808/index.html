808 Voice Bench

All sixteen TR-808 voices from the 1981 service notes, each in four versions. Circuit simulates every resistor, capacitor, diode, transistor and op-amp on the schematic, sample by sample. Cached plays hits the circuit rendered in advance, at almost no CPU (level and decay work, pitch and tone don't). To cache your own sound, set a voice's knobs and press its Cache button: the circuit renders fresh hits at those settings and Cached plays them from then on (kept in this browser; Revert goes back). Synth rebuilds the same sound from a few oscillators, filters and envelopes fitted to the circuit model, at a fraction of the CPU. 808 plays recordings of a real TR-808 at the nearest knob setting.

This computer can't run all the circuit models in real time, so you may hear dropouts. Switch some voices to Synth or 808; CY, MA, CP, SD and CB cost the most.

Click a step to cycle off → on → accent. The loaded pattern is an example.

Each voice lives in its own files under voices/: sd.js is the synth snare, sd-circuit.js the circuit one, with the schematic references and part values in its header comment. The circuit solver (modified nodal analysis with Newton iteration) is dsp/mna.js.

The 808 recordings are Michael Fischer's TR-808 sample set (1994, serial no. 103852): dry, from each voice's own output, LEVEL full, every other knob at 0, 2.5, 5, 7.5 and 10. The set has no accented hits, so Accent scales the recording by the circuit model's accent ratio. See cache/808/SOURCE.md.

The circuit models are heavy: a busy pattern can need more than one CPU core. The DSP load readout shows how close you are.

Closed hi-hat chokes the open hi-hat, as Q23 does on the voicing board. CB, CY, OH and CH share the same free-running oscillators, so no two metal hits start in the same phase.
