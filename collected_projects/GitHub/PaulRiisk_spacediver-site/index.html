Stand still to shoot. Move to dodge. You can't do both.

A top-down roguelite built around one rule. Your weapon locks onto the nearest enemy and fires while you hold position. Move, and it stops. Every room is a question of where to stand, when to break cover, and which shot is worth the hit you'll take for it.

Random builds every run, permanent progression between them. Lose a run, keep what you unlocked.
