About Us

Manufacturing building materials for Gunamangalam and beyond

Velorr Industries is a building-material manufacturing business established in August 2024, based at Gunamangalam, Cuddalore District, Tamil Nadu.

We manufacture Fly Ash Bricks, Hollow Blocks and Red Bricks at our facility, with compression testing carried out as part of our quality process. We are a GST-registered manufacturer, and transport is available for delivery to project sites.

  • Manufacturing of Fly Ash Bricks, Hollow Blocks and Red Bricks at our own facility
  • Compression testing carried out on products
  • Support for bulk requirements from contractors, builders and dealers
  • Delivery transport available to project sites
