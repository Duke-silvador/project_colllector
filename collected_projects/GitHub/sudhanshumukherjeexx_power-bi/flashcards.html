Power BI interview flashcards

How these cards work

You don't need Power BI open to use these. Read the question and say your answer out loud, then flip the card and compare. Got it schedules the card for later: tomorrow, then 3, 7, 14 and 30 days. Review again brings it back in this session. Come back daily and study Due today. Tap any dotted-underlined term for a plain-English definition.

Mock interview

Questions come from the deck, level and category you've selected. You get one question at a time with a timer. Answer out loud as if the interviewer were in the room, then reveal the model answer and mark yourself honestly. Your marks feed the same spaced-repetition schedule as the cards.
