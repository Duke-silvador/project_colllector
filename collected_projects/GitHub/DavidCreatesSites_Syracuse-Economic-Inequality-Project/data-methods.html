Project DetailsSources and methods

Read the measure before comparing the number

Each chart records its geography, period, units, source, manuscript pages, and review status. City, school-district, county, and metropolitan data are kept distinct. A count is not a rate; mean income is not median income; income is not wealth.

Interactive controls let readers inspect values, change measures where available, sort categories, and download data with its source information. The underlying tables remain readable without JavaScript. The maps are provided by CNYVitals and require an internet connection.

Some manuscript values still require independent confirmation. Missing margins of error and incomplete Opportunity Atlas settings are disclosed, and unsupported causal claims are not presented as findings.
