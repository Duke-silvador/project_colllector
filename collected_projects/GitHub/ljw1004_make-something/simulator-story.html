MAKE SOMETHING WORTHWHILE (story engine)

1. COOKBOOK. Write a cookbook. Make doughs, sauces, fillings, pastries and cakes.

2. CREATION. Create the world, and every living creature that moves upon it.

3. HISTORY OF IDEAS. Follow questions across centuries: what explains an eclipse, what makes a life good, and when should a rule change? Combine evidence, arguments and inventions to discover how ideas meet—and what each encounter makes possible.

4. MISSION TO MARS. Your lander crashes badly onto the surface. Can you patch things up and complete your science mission?

5. SETTLEMENT. You are a settler at the dawn of history. Found your land's first settlement.

6. THEOLOGY. You are Augustine, Aquinas, Martin Luther. How will you respond to the arguments of your day? Why does evil exist? Can we do good without God’s help? What happens at Communion? Replay these great debates from their perspectives. Share their struggles to reconcile faith, reason, and the ideas of their time.
