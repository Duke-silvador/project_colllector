Privacy Policy

Effective September 5, 2026

Data we process

The public site can be used without creating an account. Basic server logs may contain request time, technical request data and IP information needed for security and reliability. We do not sell personal data.

Third-party platforms

Links and embeds from YouTube, TikTok, Spotify, Instagram, X and other platforms may be subject to those services' own privacy policies when you interact with them.

Artist data

Public artist information is assembled from verified official accounts and public/open data sources. NUGU RADAR does not publish private messages or private-account content.

Retention and security

Operational logs and metric snapshots are retained only as needed for service reliability and trend calculation. Credentials are stored server-side and are never exposed in the public frontend.

Contact

Privacy or correction requests may be submitted through the project contact channel published on suaveforge.com.
