Homelab

Homelab is the sign-in in front of the private, self-hosted services under platanosverdes.com. Only people who have been invited can open them.

How sign-in works

You can sign in with Google or with a code sent to your email. Your email address is only used to check whether you have been invited, and to show who is signed in on the service you open. It is never sold or shared.

Details are in the privacy policy.
