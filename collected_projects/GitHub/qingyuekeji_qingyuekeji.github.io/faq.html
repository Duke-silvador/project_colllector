Frequently Asked Questions

Everything buyers usually ask before placing an eco packaging order.

What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ)?
MOQ depends on the product and the finish. For standard unprinted items it is typically 50,000–100,000 pcs per size; for custom-printed items usually 50,000 pcs per design. Mixed sizes are possible in some cases — send us your target quantity and we will confirm the exact MOQ.
Can I get free samples?
Yes. We provide stock samples free of charge; you only cover the courier cost. For custom-printed or custom-size samples, a sampling fee and mould/plate cost may apply, which is normally refundable against a bulk order.
What is the lead time?
Stock/unprinted items: usually 15–25 days after deposit. Custom-printed items: usually 25–35 days after artwork approval and deposit. Peak season (Aug–Nov) may be longer — we will confirm the exact schedule with your order.
What payment terms do you accept?
Standard terms are 30% deposit by T/T and the balance before shipment. For long-term partners we can discuss L/C at sight or other terms. All payments are made to the company account only.
Which shipping terms (Incoterms) do you support?
We normally quote FOB (nearest port) and can also arrange CIF, CFR, DDP or EXW. FOB is recommended for your first orders as it keeps risk and cost clear.
Do your products have certifications?
Our partner manufacturing base holds ISO 9001, BSCI, FSC, FSSC 22000, HACCP and OK Compost HOME (for qualifying compostable items). Certificate copies and test reports can be provided on request; where required we can also arrange third-party testing for your market.
Are the products food-safe?
Yes. Products are made from food-grade paper and food-contact compliant materials, produced under FSSC 22000 / HACCP systems in dust-free workshops. We can supply migration/food-contact test reports for your destination market.
Are the products really biodegradable or compostable?
Paper cups, bowls, boxes, plates and bags are paper-based and recyclable/biodegradable. Straws are available in paper, PP, PHA and PLA. Home-compostable claims apply only to specific certified items (e.g. OK Compost HOME) — we will tell you exactly which items carry which claim.
Can you print my logo / do private label?
Yes. We support custom printing (up to 6 colours), embossing, hot stamping, matte/gloss finishing, private label and custom packaging. Send us your artwork in AI/PDF/EPS and we will confirm printability and provide a digital mock-up.
How do you control quality?
Incoming material inspection, in-process checks (every 30 minutes), UV sterilisation (1 hour), oven drying (8 hours) and final inspection before shipment. We can also welcome third-party inspection (SGS/BV/Intertek) at your request.
Can I visit the factory?
Yes, we welcome factory visits. Our partner manufacturing base is located in Dongguan, Guangdong. Please contact us in advance so we can arrange the schedule and, if needed, an interpreter.
What if there is a quality problem after delivery?
Please send photos/video and the batch information within 30 days of receipt. Verified quality issues are handled by replacement, credit note or refund according to our agreement; the manufacturing party bears the cost of confirmed production defects.
How do we start working together?
1) Send your product, size, quantity and target market; 2) we reply with specifications, MOQ, price and lead time; 3) free stock samples or paid custom samples; 4) artwork and order confirmation; 5) deposit, production, inspection, shipment, after-sales.
