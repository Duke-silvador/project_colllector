Schools — check the catchment before you sign the lease

A lease makes an address, and the address sets the school — so in Half Moon Bay, check the catchment before you sign, not after. Seven schools sit inside the community itself, at least one in every board: Half Moon Bay Public School (3525 River Run Avenue) and Wazoson Public School (989 Kilbirnie Drive, opened January 2024, its name meaning “bird’s nest” in Algonquin/Anishinaabe) in the public board; St. Benedict School (2525 River Mist Road), St. Cecilia School (3490 Cambrian Road) and St. Juan Diego School (135 Main Halyard Lane, opened 2024) in the Catholic board; École élémentaire catholique Sainte-Kateri (2450 River Mist Road); and École élémentaire publique Des Visionnaires (110 Robin Easey Avenue). One caution matters: both English boards split the community by street, roughly along Cambrian Road and the realigned Greenbank, so your exact school follows your exact address. Students feed Cedarview Middle School (2760 Cedarview Road) and John McCrae Secondary School (103 Malvern Drive) in the public board, St. Joseph High School (3333 Greenbank Road) in the Catholic board, École secondaire catholique Pierre-Savard (1110 Longfields Drive) and École secondaire publique Pierre-de-Blois (1310 Chapman Mills Drive) — and a funded public secondary school is coming to 3853 Cambrian Road. Zones are set by address and do shift: confirm yours with the board’s school locator before you sign, or tell me the school first and I will confirm the catchment for any specific address.

Parks, trails and recreation

Renting a house here buys you the community’s recreation anchor without the mortgage: the Minto Recreation Complex — Barrhaven (3500 Cambrian Road, at the Cambrian–Greenbank circle) carries a six-lane 25 m lap pool, a leisure pool with a lazy river, two NHL-sized rinks, an outdoor artificial turf field, an indoor walking track, a gymnasium and weight and cardio rooms. Half Moon Bay Park (3525 Cambrian Road) carries the splash pad and the community’s signature landmark — the “Falling Star” sculpture (Don Maynard, 2013), a 13-foot cast-aluminium star balanced on one point atop the toboggan hill — and Guinness Park (120 Ramsgrange Street) adds a second splash pad. Around them runs a deep bench of pocket parks — Black Raven Park, Cappamore Park, Celestial Park, Flagstaff Park, Regatta Park, River Mist Park, River Run Park, Watercolours Park — plus Half Moon Bay District Park, wrapped around a stormwater pond draining toward the Jock River, and the river corridor itself along the northern edge.

Getting around from Half Moon Bay

Today’s transit runs on two routes. Route 75 (Frequent) is the principal route inside the community — it runs the Cambrian and Kilbirnie interior on River Mist Road, Cappamore Drive and Greenbank Road and connects to O-Train Line 1 at Tunney’s Pasture. Route 275 (Connexion) is the peak-period express from the Half Moon Bay Road and Perseus Avenue terminus to Tunney’s Pasture. The nearest Park & Ride lots sit north of the river: Fallowfield (with the VIA Rail station), Strandherd and Nepean Woods. By car, Highway 416 is the west-side advantage — reached today at Fallowfield Road, with a full Barnsdale Road interchange designed and planned — and the Greenbank Road realignment will bring a four-lane arterial with a median bus rapid transit corridor, planned stations at Cambrian, Dundonald and Kilbirnie, and a new bridge over the Jock River in a later, funding-dependent phase. What Ottawa landlords ask for — come prepared

Ontario landlords typically ask for a credit check, proof of income or an employment letter, references from previous landlords, and first and last month’s rent as deposit — the only legal deposit in Ontario; “key money” is not permitted. Ontario’s Standard Form of Lease is mandatory for most tenancies. Have your documents ready before you tour: when a Half Moon Bay freehold rental lists, the prepared applicant wins — and I can get your application in front of the listing agent the same day.

Ontario renting essentials

Most leases start with a one-year term, then continue month-to-month. Rent control applies to units first occupied before November 15, 2018 — most of Half Moon Bay’s stock is newer, so ask whether the guideline applies to any specific house before you sign. “No pets” clauses are void under Ontario’s Residential Tenancies Act, which makes freehold houses the pet-friendly choice. Utilities are often tenant-paid; snow removal and lawn care are negotiable.
