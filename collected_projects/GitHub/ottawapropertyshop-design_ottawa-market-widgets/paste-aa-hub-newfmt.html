The model, in plain English

eXp Realty commission split, explained in plain English

Key takeaway

In Canada, eXp Realty takes 20% of your commissions until it has collected $16,000 from you in your anniversary year. After that, you keep 100% of every commission (minus small per-deal fees). That $16,000 ceiling is called the "cap" — it is the maximum the brokerage can ever take from your splits.

First, the words — before the numbers

A "split" is the percentage of each commission cheque that goes to the brokerage instead of you. An 80/20 split means you keep 80% and the brokerage keeps 20%.

A "cap" is a ceiling on the split. It is the maximum total dollar amount the brokerage can collect from your 80/20 splits in one year. Once your 20% contributions add up to the cap, the split turns off. You stop paying the 20% entirely.

"GCI" means gross commission income — the total commissions you earn before any split or fee.

An "anniversary year" is your personal 12-month cycle starting the day you join eXp, not the calendar year. Your cap resets every anniversary, not every January.

eXp Realty commission split in Canada: 80/20 to a $16,000 cap

Here is the Canadian model, verified October 2026 against eXp's official income disclosure (exprealty.ca/income):

  • Split: 80/20. You keep 80% of every commission; eXp keeps 20%.

  • Cap: $16,000 CAD per anniversary year. Because 20% of $80,000 is $16,000, the cap is reached the moment you earn $80,000 in GCI.

  • After the cap: 100% commission to you. eXp charges a $285 transaction fee on each of your next 20 deals (maximum $5,700 total), then the fee drops to $75 per deal for the rest of the year.

  • Reset: the cap resets on your anniversary date, and the cycle starts again.

eXp Realty revenue share: the 7-tier system, explained

Plain English first: what revenue share actually is

"Revenue share" means eXp shares its own revenue — the 20% company cut — with agents who help the brokerage grow. When you attract a productive agent to eXp and they pay their splits, eXp sends you a slice of what it collected. The agent you attracted loses nothing; their split and cap are identical either way. The money comes out of eXp's half of the cap flow you saw above.

A "tier" is a generation in your group. Tier 1 = agents you personally attracted. If one of them attracts an agent, that new agent sits in your Tier 2. Then Tier 3 — down to Tier 7. You earn the tier's percentage on every productive agent in all seven generations.

"FLQA" is eXp's term for a productive sponsored agent (roughly: a personally sponsored agent who earned about $5,000 in GCI in the last six months). FLQAs are the key that unlocks your deeper tiers.
