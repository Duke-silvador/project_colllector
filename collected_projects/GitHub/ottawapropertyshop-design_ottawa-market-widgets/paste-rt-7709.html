Getting around from Strandherd

Strandherd’s transit anchor is its own station: Strandherd station, on the Southwest Transitway at the pocket’s northwest corner by Greenbank Road, pairs rapid-transit service (routes 70, 75, 80, 173, 275, 279 and 305) with a Park & Ride — drive two minutes, ride the rest. What Ottawa landlords ask for — come prepared

Ontario landlords typically ask for a credit check, proof of income or an employment letter, references from previous landlords, and first and last month’s rent as deposit — the only legal deposit in Ontario; “key money” is not permitted. Ontario’s Standard Form of Lease is mandatory for most tenancies. Have your documents ready before you tour: when a Strandherd freehold rental lists, the prepared applicant wins — and I can get your application in front of the listing agent the same day.

Ontario renting essentials

Most leases start with a one-year term, then continue month-to-month. Rent control applies to units first occupied before November 15, 2018. “No pets” clauses are void under Ontario’s Residential Tenancies Act, which makes freehold houses the pet-friendly choice. Utilities are often tenant-paid; snow removal and lawn care are negotiable.
