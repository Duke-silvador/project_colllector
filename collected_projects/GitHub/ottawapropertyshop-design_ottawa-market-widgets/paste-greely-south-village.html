South Village — the community

South Village is one of the lake communities of Sunset Lakes Developments — the Greely developer founded by Dan Anderson in 1990, whose formula shaped the modern village: estate lots around a spring-fed lake that doubles as the community’s stormwater pond, design-controlled custom homes, and an owners’ association that runs the shared amenities. South Village carries that formula at the village’s northwest end, around Village Lake: the homes run along South Village Drive and Lake Forest Walk, with South Beach Boulevard and West Beach Way on the lake’s south shore, Sparkling Lake Way at the community’s north edge and Sandy Beach Court tucked by the beach.

The exact streets of South Village — every street inside the gold boundary belongs to the community, and the listings at the top of this guide are drawn from inside that same boundary.

South Village’s boundaries, approximately: Sunset Lakes edges the north, the Pebble Trail Way side of Woodstream edges the west, and the community’s south-shore streets — South Beach Boulevard and West Beach Way — close the south above the Parkway Road farmland. To the east, the boundary runs out along South Village Drive toward the Old Prescott Road bend, short of Aldergrove Way. Those boundaries are approximate: they follow the community’s own street footprint — the streets traced on the map above — rather than a surveyed line.

The community built out through the 2000s — homes on South Village Drive and Lake Forest Walk were built in 2006, 2007, 2008 and 2012 in the records — so this is a finished, settled neighbourhood, not a building site: mature landscaping, the stone entrance sign on its cedar hedge, and streets where every home is a resale proposition. What the association runs is the centre of daily life here: a clubhouse on the lake side of the courts, an outdoor pool and gym, and the tennis and pickleball courts — listing copy for homes here puts the association fee at about $350 a year for that amenity set.

Servicing in South Village — wells, septic and the lake

South Village runs on the classic Greely arrangement — the opposite of Shadow Ridge’s city systems. Each home here has its own private well and its own septic system, the standard homework on any Greely purchase: have the well water tested, have the septic system inspected, and budget for both as the private infrastructure they are. There is no municipal water or sewer bill and no communal system — the services are yours, on your lot.

The lake is part of the servicing story, in the Sunset Lakes Developments way: Village Lake is a spring-fed lake that also does the community’s stormwater work — the developer’s formula across its Greely communities, where the lake at the centre of the neighbourhood is both the view and the pond. The owners’ association holds the shared side of the community together — the clubhouse, pool, gym and courts — for the fee listing copy puts at about $350 a year.

Living in South Village

Daily life in South Village runs through the clubhouse and the lake: the tennis and pickleball courts beside the clubhouse, the pool and gym in season, and the sandy beach on Village Lake for the summer. The village core is about 1.9 km east — The Shoppes of Greely plaza on Bank Street for groceries and everyday services, the Greely Community Centre and the library branch on Meadow Drive. Bank Street (Ottawa Road 31) is the spine: downtown Ottawa is roughly half an hour north in good traffic. The honest transit picture: there is no regular OC Transpo route through the village — the one exception is the Thursday Shopper Route 304 — so South Village is a driver’s address, and school buses, run by the Ottawa Student Transportation Authority, are the transit that matters to families here.

South Village questions, answered

What is South Village?
A built-out estate community of Sunset Lakes Developments at Greely’s northwest end, wrapped around Village Lake. Its streets — South Village Drive, Lake Forest Walk, South Beach Boulevard, West Beach Way, Sparkling Lake Way and Sandy Beach Court — carry design-controlled custom homes on estate lots, most of them built through the 2000s.

What does the owners’ association run, and what does it cost?
The association runs the community’s shared amenities: the clubhouse, the outdoor pool and gym, and the tennis and pickleball courts by the lake. Listing copy for homes here puts the association fee at about $350 a year — confirm the current fee and what it covers on any specific listing before you buy.

Is South Village on city water and sewers?
No — South Village homes run on private wells and private septic systems, the classic Greely arrangement. That is the opposite of Shadow Ridge and Lakeland Meadows, Greely’s city-serviced pocket. On a South Village purchase, the homework is a well-water test and a septic inspection.

Which schools serve South Village?
The designated paths are set out in the schools section above: Greely Elementary School and Castor Valley Elementary School on the public side, Osgoode Township High School for Grades 9 to 12, and St. Mary School to St. Mark Catholic High School on the Catholic side. Confirm any exact address in the board’s school locator before you buy.

How far is South Village from downtown Ottawa?
About 25 kilometres — roughly half an hour by car in good traffic, up Parkway Road to Bank Street (Ottawa Road 31). There is no regular OC Transpo route through the village; the Thursday Shopper Route 304 is the one exception.
