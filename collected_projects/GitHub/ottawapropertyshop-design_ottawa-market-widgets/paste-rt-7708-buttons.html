What actually comes up for rent in Stonebridge

Stonebridge was built primarily in the 2000s — the first phases around 2000, the bulk from 2004 to 2010 — by Minto, Monarch, Mattamy, Uniform and Tamarack, Cardel and Phoenix, and its rental shelf is that stock turning over: owners posted away, investors holding executive product, households between homes. Most Stonebridge rental listings are four-bedroom detached homes with double garages, many advertising the fairway behind them. Below that band sit the townhouses on the quiet crescents — the realistic entry into the golf-course community — and the bungalows, 1,400 to 2,600+ square feet on 45-to-52-foot frontages, that rent to downsizers who want main-floor living without buying it. Two Stonebridge specifics belong in every renter's homework. The first is the Stonebridge Special Area Levy — about $48 per $100K of assessed value, a landlord cost line scheduled to end in October 2029; it can matter in renewal negotiations as that end date approaches, so know it before a multi-year lease. The second is the long-running question of development on parts of the golf-course lands: proposals surfaced years ago and the City held a community information session in 2019, but the current status is unverified — if a fairway view is why you are renting a specific home, ask about it before you sign.

Schools — check the catchment before you sign the lease

A lease makes an address, and the address sets the school — so in Stonebridge, check the catchment before you sign, not after. Adrienne Clarkson Elementary School (JK–6, 170 Stoneway Drive) anchors the school story — triple-track (Regular English, Early French Immersion, Middle French Immersion), about 435 students, opened in 2001, on a large property beside Stonecrest Park with shared soccer pitches, baseball diamonds, play structures and a community rink. Getting around from Stonebridge

By car, downtown Ottawa runs about 25–30 minutes off-peak, with Greenbank Road and Prince of Wales Drive as the main routes out and Highway 416 reached via Strandherd Drive. Transit is edge-based: the Frequent Route 75 runs along Greenbank Road on the community's western edge to Cambrian Road and the Minto Recreation Complex — extended south of Kilbirnie Drive via River Mist Road, Cappamore Drive and Greenbank Road — and the 600-series school routes serve the area. The nearest Park & Rides are Strandherd (3680 Strandherd Drive) and Fallowfield. Renters on the interior crescents should check their exact stop before counting on a walk-to-bus commute.

What Ottawa landlords ask for — come prepared

Ontario landlords typically ask for a credit check, proof of income or an employment letter, references from previous landlords, and first and last month's rent as deposit — the only legal deposit in Ontario; "key money" is not permitted. Ontario's Standard Form of Lease is mandatory for most tenancies. In Stonebridge, also confirm how the special area levy is treated — it is the landlord's cost line, and it phases out in October 2029. Have your documents ready before you tour: when a Stonebridge freehold rental lists, the prepared applicant wins — and I can get your application in front of the listing agent the same day.

Ontario renting essentials

Most leases start with a one-year term, then continue month-to-month. Rent control applies to units first occupied before November 15, 2018. "No pets" clauses are void under Ontario's Residential Tenancies Act, which makes freehold houses the pet-friendly choice. Utilities are often tenant-paid; snow removal and lawn care are negotiable.
