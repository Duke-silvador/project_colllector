Why rent in Heritage Park

Most Barrhaven pockets are either brand-new subdivisions or older stock. Heritage Park sits in the sweet spot: an established 1990s–early-2000s neighbourhood with mature landscaping, solid Minto and Caivan construction, and streets like Cashmere Terrace, Harthill Way, Kennevale Drive, and Mountshannon Drive that renters actively ask for by name. Its distinctive green feature — the Fraser Fields and Harthill Way linear parks built on the decommissioned former Strandherd Drive alignment — gives the pocket a park network most suburbs don't have. For renters who want a family home with space and character instead of a cookie-cutter new build, this is the Barrhaven pocket.

Housing stock and rent bands

Heritage Park's rental stock centres on Minto Haven-model townhomes and Caivan-built detached homes from the 1990s–2000s — spacious modern layouts with 3- to 4-bedroom plans — plus low-rise walk-up condo apartments on Harthill Way, Anthracite Private, and Chromite Private for renters who want condo-style living in a family neighbourhood. Across Barrhaven, townhouses and semis typically lease between $2,200 and $2,800 per month while detached houses generally start near $2,800 — and Heritage Park's larger 1990s–2000s floor plans put it comfortably mid-to-upper band.

Commuting from a Heritage Park rental

OC Transpo routes 171, 173, and 99 serve the pocket along Longfields Drive and the Strandherd corridor, with school routes 673/699 covering Pierre-de-Blois. By car, this is the closest Barrhaven sub-area to Highway 416 — off-peak drives downtown run roughly 25–30 minutes via Strandherd and the 416. Parks, trails, and recreation

Heritage Park's signature greenspace is the Harthill Way Linear Park and Fraser Fields Parkette, created from the decommissioned former Strandherd Drive alignment — a genuine linear park network through the neighbourhood. Barrhaven Mews Park and Clarity Park (splash pad) are nearby, and Jock River trails are within walking distance. The Minto Recreation Complex – Barrhaven at 3500 Cambrian Road is the area's flagship facility: 25m pool, lazy river, two NHL-sized arenas, gym with walking track, and a CFL/FIFA-dimension turf field. Cineplex Odeon Barrhaven on Riocan Avenue is a short drive.

Shopping and everyday life

Heritage Park itself is mostly residential, which keeps it quiet — but everything is close. The Barrhaven Town Centre and RioCan Marketplace corridors (Loblaws, Walmart, Home Depot, Shoppers Drug Mart, Cineplex, restaurants) are about three minutes away, and Costco Barrhaven at 4315 Strandherd Drive is closer from here than from any other Barrhaven sub-area.

What Ottawa landlords ask for — come prepared

Ontario landlords typically ask for a credit check, proof of income or an employment letter, references from previous landlords, and first and last month's rent as deposit — the only legal deposit in Ontario; "key money" is not permitted. Ontario's Standard Form of Lease is mandatory for most tenancies. Have your documents ready before you tour: the best freehold rentals lease in under a month, and I can get your application in front of the listing agent the same day.

Ontario renting essentials

Most leases start with a one-year term, then continue month-to-month. Rent control applies to units first occupied before November 15, 2018. "No pets" clauses are void under Ontario's Residential Tenancies Act, which makes freehold houses the pet-friendly choice. Utilities are often tenant-paid; snow removal and lawn care are negotiable.
