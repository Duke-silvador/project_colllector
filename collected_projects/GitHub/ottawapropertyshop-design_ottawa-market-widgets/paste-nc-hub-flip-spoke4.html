Rebate Math Decoded: Up to $130,000 — and a Deadline That Will Not Move

Federal first-time-buyer GST rebate — up to $50,000. The federal rebate covers the 5% GST portion for first-time buyers on new homes up to $1,000,000, with a linear phase-out to $1,500,000.
