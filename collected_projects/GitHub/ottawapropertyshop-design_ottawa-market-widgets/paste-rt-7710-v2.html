Getting around from Barrhaven East

Barrhaven East renters get two Transitway anchors at the pocket’s edges: Beatrice station on Chapman Mills Drive, and Nepean Woods station — the Park & Ride at Woodroffe Avenue and Strandherd Drive. What Ottawa landlords ask for — come prepared

Ontario landlords typically ask for a credit check, proof of income or an employment letter, references from previous landlords, and first and last month’s rent as deposit — the only legal deposit in Ontario; “key money” is not permitted. Ontario’s Standard Form of Lease is mandatory for most tenancies. Have your documents ready before you tour: when a Barrhaven East freehold rental lists, the prepared applicant wins — and I can get your application in front of the listing agent the same day.

Ontario renting essentials

Most leases start with a one-year term, then continue month-to-month. Rent control applies to units first occupied before November 15, 2018. “No pets” clauses are void under Ontario’s Residential Tenancies Act, which makes freehold houses the pet-friendly choice. Utilities are often tenant-paid; snow removal and lawn care are negotiable.
