This guide to the Heritage Park (7704) neighbourhood starts with the real numbers — the schools, parks and price bands that matter — because streets here trade on school catchments and the move-in-ready layouts of its established 1990s and 2000s homes.

Heritage Park is Barrhaven's 1990s–early-2000s family pocket (AMP area 7704 — Barrhaven — Heritage Park) — centred on Strandherd Drive and Longfields Drive and built around spacious, modern layouts. It takes in the large Fraser Fields community by Holitzner Homes and Tartan Homes (streets like Harthill Way and Broxburn), plus Minto's Haven-model townhomes and Caivan detached homes, and it holds the strongest school cluster of the three northern pockets in this guide series. Its signature green feature is unlike anything else in Barrhaven: the Fraser Fields Parkette and Harthill Way Linear Park, built on the decommissioned former Strandherd Drive alignment. And it is the closest of the three to Costco Barrhaven and Highway 416.

Commute & Transportation

OC Transpo route 171 runs the Longfields corridor (Longfields/Mother Teresa H.S., Longfields/Beatrice, Longfields/Woodroffe); route 173 serves Strandherd/Liebe, Strandherd/Fraser Fields and Strandherd/Kingsview; route 99 covers both corridors (Strandherd/Fraser Fields, Longfields/Chapman Mills, Marketplace/Longfields); school routes 673 and 699 serve Pierre-de-Blois via the Strandherd corridor. The VIA Rail corridor crosses Strandherd Road just south of the pocket — worth knowing if you are sensitive to train noise near the crossing; the long-term plan converts the Transitway to LRT with a Strandherd station. Road access is the pocket's edge: quick Highway 416 access, about 3 minutes to the Barrhaven Town Centre and RioCan Marketplace, and a typical 25–30 minute drive to downtown Ottawa off-peak.

Parks, Trails & Recreation

The distinctive green feature: Harthill Way Linear Park (125 Harthill Way) and Fraser Fields Parkette (309 Harthill Way) — both created from the decommissioned former Strandherd Drive alignment, per City of Ottawa planning documents. Nearby: Barrhaven Mews Park and Clarity Park (splash pad). And the Jock River trails sit within walking distance (per listing copy) — the pocket's water-adjacent angle. For big community moments, Clarke Fields Park (93 Houlahan Street) hosts Barrhaven's multi-day Canada Day festival with fireworks — the suburb's signature summer event. The Minto Recreation Complex — Barrhaven (3500 Cambrian Road: 25m pool, two NHL rinks, gymnasium, walking track) is the nearest major recreation facility — full details in the Half Moon Bay guide — Barrhaven's new-construction hub.

Clarke Fields Park, 93 Houlahan Street — Barrhaven's Canada Day festival grounds, at the edge of Heritage Park.
