Knollsbrook is Barrhaven's transit pocket — and it wears the title literally. OC Transpo route 173 runs through its interior streets (Foxfield, Knollsbrook, Wolfgang, Holitman), the Fallowfield VIA station and Park & Ride sit immediately to the north, and Barrhaven Public School stands inside the pocket on Larkin Drive. This wedge-shaped cluster east of Greenbank Road, between Fallowfield Road and the railway tracks (AMP area 7702), pairs quality-built (Holitzner) detached homes with townhouses and the South Station apartment condos — the most entry-friendly price band of the three northern Barrhaven pockets in this guide series.

If you are browsing Ottawa houses for sale and want Barrhaven where the bus stops are on your own street and the VIA station is a walk away, this Knollsbrook neighbourhood guide covers the schools, the routes, the parks and the price context that matter. Groceries and pharmacy will likely need a car — everything else is closer than you think.

Schools & Catchments

Barrhaven Public School (80 Larkin Drive, OCDSB, JK–6) sits inside the pocket on Larkin Drive — the neighbourhood's own elementary school. Nearby options include John McCrae Secondary, Mother Teresa High School, St. Patrick Catholic Elementary School, Berrigan Elementary School and École Terre-des-Jeunes. Catchments are still being mapped address by address — specifically whether Barrhaven Public or St. Patrick Catholic ES (68 Larkin Drive) serves which streets — so confirm your address in the board finders before buying.

Commute & Transportation

This is Knollsbrook's headline. OC Transpo route 173 (Bayshore – Barrhaven Centre) serves stops inside the pocket: Foxfield/Knollsbrook, Foxfield/Holitman, Wolfgang/Fallowfield, Wolfgang/Foxfield, Fallowfield/Holitman and Fallowfield/Fallowfield Station. At the pocket's edge, the Greenbank/Larkin stop carries routes 70, 75 and 173 (first bus ~4:42 AM, last ~11:03 PM–12:02 AM) plus school routes 677, 696, 671, 672, 682 and 685. Immediately north: the Fallowfield VIA Rail station and Fallowfield Park & Ride (3347 Fallowfield Road) — Knollsbrook is the closest pocket in this batch to the station. Road access is via the Greenbank Road and Fallowfield Road arterials, with a typical 25–30 minute drive to downtown Ottawa off-peak.

Parks, Trails & Recreation

Neill-Nesbitt Park (70 Foxfield — Foxfield is an interior street) offers a puddle rink in winter; Greenbank Park (895 Greenbank Road) brings the splash pad. Listing copy for the pocket highlights the splash pad, soccer fields, baseball diamond and basketball court across the two parks — a compact but complete neighbourhood recreation set.

Shopping, Groceries & Restaurants

No large-format retail sits inside the pocket itself — it is residential only, with shopping a short drive to the Barrhaven Town Centre and Marketplace corridors. One honest buyer note: groceries and a pharmacy will likely need a car from here (including from the South Station condos). Housing Stock & Price Context

The wedge-shaped pocket east of Greenbank Road — Fallowfield Road to the north, the railway tracks to the south — holds established, mature streets: quality-built Holitzner detached homes (a 4-bed/3-bath Holitzner was an active listing here), townhouses on Knollsbrook Drive and Foxfield Drive, and the South Station apartment condos (3684 Fallowfield Road) — built 2012/2013 by Tega Homes, 3 floors, 57 units, with an exercise room, elevators, storage lockers and underground parking; the Greenbank/Larkin bus stop is about a four-minute walk. Price context, shown here only as aggregate bands: this is the most affordable of the three northern pockets in this series. List bands: 1-bed apartment condo ~$299,900; townhouses ~$359,000–$359,999; semis in the ~$523/sq ft range (Aug–Oct 2025 aggregate); detached listings up to ~$899,900. Pricing moves with the market — ask me for current comparable sales before you write an offer.

Services, Healthcare & Daycare

Named services on the pocket's edge: the Barrhaven Appletree Walk-In Clinic (900 Greenbank Road), the Mountain Goat Yoga Centre (3350 Fallowfield Road), and Douvris Martial Arts and Fitness near the Greenbank Park splash pad. Daycare options at the Fallowfield station area serve the pocket: Little Scholars Montessori Barrhaven (Location 2) and BrightPath Fallowfield Child Care Centre (both 3500 Fallowfield Road). No named restaurants were confirmed inside the pocket — nearest food options are in the Greenbank/Fallowfield plazas. Barrhaven's full healthcare directory belongs on the parent Barrhaven page.

Churches & Community

Good Shepherd Barrhaven (Anglican Lutheran, 3780 Fallowfield Road) sits near the Fallowfield station area, adjacent to the pocket; no place of worship was confirmed inside the 7702 boundaries. No Knollsbrook-specific community association was found — Barrhaven-wide representation applies. Both points will be re-verified against the final area boundary before this page publishes.

Safety & Street Character

An established, mature pocket with quality-built detached homes and quiet interior streets — the kind of neighbourhood where the bus stops are named after your street. (No neighbourhood-specific crime statistics were found; Barrhaven-wide safety context lives on the parent page.)

Future Development

Knollsbrook is essentially built out — no active new-build project was identified inside the pocket. Change comes from infill and renovations, not new subdivisions, which is part of why the pocket's character and price band hold steady. Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Knollsbrook in Barrhaven?
Knollsbrook (AMP area 7702) is the wedge-shaped pocket east of Greenbank Road, bounded by Fallowfield Road to the north and the railway tracks to the south — immediately south of the Fallowfield VIA station.

What schools are near Knollsbrook, Ottawa?
Barrhaven Public School (80 Larkin Dr, JK–6) sits inside the pocket; nearby options include John McCrae Secondary, Mother Teresa High School, St. Patrick Catholic ES, Berrigan ES and École Terre-des-Jeunes. Confirm catchments with the board finders.

How do I get downtown from Knollsbrook by transit?
Route 173 runs through the pocket's interior streets (Foxfield, Knollsbrook, Wolfgang, Holitman); routes 70, 75 and 173 serve the Greenbank/Larkin stop; the Fallowfield Park & Ride (3347 Fallowfield Rd) is immediately north.

What types of homes are in Knollsbrook?
