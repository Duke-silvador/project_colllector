Schools in Half Moon Bay

Schools are this community’s signature strength: Half Moon Bay has an in-community school in every board — no other Barrhaven sub-area can say that. English public families choose between Half Moon Bay Public School and Wazoson Public School, which opened in January 2024 — its name means “bird’s nest” in Algonquin/Anishinaabe, and an attached child-care centre shares the site. English Catholic families have three in-community elementaries — St. Benedict, St. Cecilia and St. Juan Diego, which also opened in 2024. French Catholic students attend École élémentaire catholique Sainte-Kateri, and French public students attend École élémentaire publique Des Visionnaires, both inside the community. Older students feed to schools just north of the river for now — and a funded public secondary school is planned inside Half Moon Bay itself, at Cambrian Road, with a scheduled opening of September 2029. One caution that matters: both English boards split this community by street, so your exact school follows your exact address — always confirm on the board locator before you buy.

Parks, trails and recreation

The anchor is the Minto Recreation Complex — Barrhaven, on Cambrian Road: a six-lane lap pool with diving boards, leisure pools, two NHL-sized rinks, an outdoor artificial-turf field, an indoor walking track and a gymnasium. Beside it, Half Moon Bay Park carries the community’s splash pad and its landmark — the “Falling Star” sculpture, a 13-foot cast-aluminium star balanced on one point atop the toboggan hill, with light shimmering through hundreds of small holes in its surface at night. Guinness Park adds a second splash pad, a deep bench of pocket parks serves every section, and the Jock River itself is the community’s northern green edge — a protected corridor along the south bank.

Shopping and daily life

Retail now exists inside the community: Food Basics is open on Cambrian Road — Barrhaven South’s first grocery store, with a full-service pharmacy — at the community core where Cambrian meets the realigned Greenbank. The big weekly runs still go north across the Jock River, minutes away: Chapman Mills Marketplace, Barrhaven Town Centre and Strandherd Crossing cover the rest. And the community is still growing: the Greenbank Road realignment will bring a new arterial with a bus rapid transit corridor and stations at Cambrian, Dundonald and Kilbirnie, and a future Highway 416 interchange at Barnsdale Road is in design study — this is the closest of Barrhaven’s eleven sub-areas to the 416.

Something interesting about Half Moon Bay

Even the street names look up: this is a community of Watercolours Way, Moonglade Crescent and Aphelion Crescent — nature- and sky-themed names wrapped around crescents, circles, pocket parks and stormwater ponds. The land was farmland within living memory; Mattamy launched Half Moon Bay in 2007, and nearly everything here is 21st-century stock, still growing into the Barrhaven South plan.

Coming up around Half Moon Bay (checked October 24, 2026)

Monday, October 26: Ottawa municipal election day. Barrhaven voters choose the mayor — 14 candidates are on the mayoral ballot — along with their ward councillor. If you live here, this is the vote that shapes the roads, transit and parks above.
