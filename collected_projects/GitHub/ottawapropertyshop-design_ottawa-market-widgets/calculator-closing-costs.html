This total is net of HST — it assumes the rebate has been assigned to the builder, so the builder receives it from the government on your behalf. That is how builders advertise new-home prices: the sticker is the after-rebate price. If you claim the rebate yourself instead, you pay the full HST at closing and the government refunds you afterwards — the same destination, a different route.
How one builder works the rebate — an example. Some builders advertise a sticker price that already includes the HST, with a $24,000 rebate credit built in. The rest of the rebate is not deducted at the sales office — you pay the sticker, then claim the remainder after closing. Worked backwards, the sticker tells you the real price.
