The sports centre at the heart of it

Most suburbs have a park at their centre; Pheasant Run has the Walter Baker Sports Centre. Opened in 1980 as the South Nepean Centre and renamed in 1983 for Walter Baker, the Nepean–Carleton MP, the complex on Malvern Drive is a full city recreation centre: an eight-lane 25-metre lap pool with a dive tank, teaching and preschool pools, two full-size NHL rinks that stay iced through the summer, gyms, and four squash courts. John McCrae Secondary School has been attached to it since the school was built in 1999, and the Ruth E. Dickinson branch of the Ottawa Public Library sits inside the same building — pool, rinks, school and library in one cluster, a short walk from most Pheasant Run streets. No other Barrhaven pocket has an anchor like it, and it is the first reason buyers give me for choosing these streets.

Schools in Pheasant Run

Ten schools sit within about three kilometres. Closest to home: Jockvale Elementary School (JK–6) and John McCrae Secondary School (9–12) serve the pocket directly, with Barrhaven Public School, Mary Honeywell Elementary School and Cedarview Middle School close by, and Longfields-Davidson Heights Secondary School for grades 7–12. Catholic options include St. Elizabeth Ann Seton and St. Patrick elementary schools and St. Mother Teresa High School, and École élémentaire catholique Pierre-Elliott-Trudeau covers French Catholic elementary. One caution that matters: catchments split by street, so your exact school follows your exact address — always confirm on the board locator before you buy.

Parks and the canopy

Pheasant Run Park is the neighbourhood’s green heart — playgrounds, walking trails, picnic space and an off-leash dog area under the mature trees. That canopy is the pocket’s signature: more than forty years of growth over the crescents, the first thing every buyer notices on the drive-through, and the one thing no new subdivision can sell you. The photograph on this page could stand in for a dozen Pheasant Run streets in late October. For the water, Jock River Landing on Prince of Wales Drive is a short drive south.

Shopping and daily life

There is no large-format retail inside Pheasant Run itself — it is a settled residential pocket, and that is the point of it. Daily life runs about five minutes out: Barrhaven Town Centre, Chapman Mills Marketplace and the Marketplace Avenue district carry the grocery runs, big-box stores, banks, clinics and pharmacies, and Greenbank and Fallowfield Roads are the spines north to the Transitway and the Greenbelt. OC Transpo routes 70 and 75 serve the pocket, the Strandherd Park & Ride is under three kilometres away, and the Fallowfield VIA station puts the Toronto–Ottawa corridor at the neighbourhood’s edge.
