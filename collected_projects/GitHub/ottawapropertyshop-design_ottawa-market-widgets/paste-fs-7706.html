Longfields is Barrhaven's big 1990s–2000s residential belt — bounded by Greenbank Road to the west, Woodroffe Avenue to the east, Fallowfield Road and the CNR railway to the north and Strandherd Drive to the south — and it holds two anchors no other Barrhaven sub-area can match: Longfields Station, the only Transitway station that sits inside its own neighbourhood, and the Longfields-Davidson Heights Secondary School campus on Berrigan Drive, which gives the area its name (AMP area 7706 — Barrhaven — Longfields). About 19,800 people live here across 4.6 km² of family streets built mostly from the early 1990s onward.

Schools & Catchments

The school roster is the headline here: Longfields-Davidson Heights Secondary School (149 Berrigan Drive, OCDSB, 7–12, about 2,250 students — Barrhaven's most recent high school) shares its campus with Berrigan Elementary School (OCDSB, JK–6); St. Mother Teresa High School (440 Longfields Drive, OCSB, 7–12) and St. Luke Catholic Elementary School (60 Mountshannon Drive, OCSB, JK–6); Monsignor Paul Baxter School (OCSB, JK–6); the French-catholic École Pierre-Elliott-Trudeau (601 prom. Longfields) and École secondaire catholique Pierre-Savard (1110 Longfields Drive, 7–12); the French-public École élémentaire publique Michaëlle-Jean (JK–8); plus the private Ottawa Christian School (255 Tartan Drive, JK–8) nearby. Eight schools inside one sub-area, across all four boards — confirm your exact catchment in the board finders before buying, because boundaries here follow street-level lines.

Commute & Transportation

Longfields Station opened April 17, 2011 on the Southwest Transitway, on the south side of the CNR/VIA rail corridor that edges the pocket's north side. Route 75 (Cambrian ↔ Tunney's Pasture) is the frequent spine and runs around the clock; route 173 is the local, Connexion routes 275 and 279 run peak-period downtown service, shopper route 305 and event routes 406/456 round out the station's list, and school routes serve the Berrigan campus. Look for Bellwether at the station — life-sized bronze sheep and a border collie by Erin Robertson and Anna Williams, herding across the station's green roof since 2011. The long-term plan converts this Transitway corridor to LRT, with Longfields one of the four Barrhaven stations converted (City of Ottawa Barrhaven LRT study). Fallowfield Station (Transitway + VIA Rail) sits on the pocket's northern edge. By road: Greenbank, Woodroffe, Fallowfield and Strandherd frame the pocket, Highway 416 is minutes west, and downtown Ottawa is a typical 25–30 minute drive off-peak.
