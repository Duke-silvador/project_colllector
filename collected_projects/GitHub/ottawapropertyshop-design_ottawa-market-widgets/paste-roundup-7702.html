The transit pocket

Knollsbrook wears its title literally. OC Transpo route 173 runs through the pocket’s interior streets — Foxfield, Knollsbrook, Wolfgang and Holitman — so the bus stops are named after the streets themselves, and the Greenbank and Larkin stop at the pocket’s edge carries the cross-town routes as well, with the first bus out at about 4:42 in the morning. Immediately north of the pocket sit the Fallowfield VIA Rail station and the Fallowfield Park & Ride: Knollsbrook is the closest Barrhaven pocket to the station, which puts Toronto and Montreal trains at the end of the street, in practical terms. On the road, the Greenbank Road and Fallowfield Road arterials frame the wedge, with a typical 25 to 30 minute drive to downtown Ottawa off-peak.

Schools in Knollsbrook

Barrhaven Public School (kindergarten to grade 6) sits inside the pocket on Larkin Drive — the neighbourhood’s own elementary school, walkable from every interior street. Around it, the nearby bench covers every board: John McCrae Secondary School and Mother Teresa High School for secondary, St. Patrick Catholic Elementary School and Berrigan Elementary School on the Catholic and public sides, and École Terre-des-Jeunes for French-language elementary. One caution that matters everywhere in Barrhaven: catchments follow your exact address, so always confirm on the board locator before you buy.

Parks and play

Two parks carry the pocket’s recreation. Foxfield Park, on an interior street, offers the green space and a puddle rink in winter — the kind of rink that exists because neighbours flood it. Greenbank Park, at the pocket’s edge, brings the splash pad, soccer fields, a baseball diamond and a basketball court. It is a compact set, but a complete one: a rink, a splash pad and three field sports inside a pocket you can walk across in fifteen minutes.

Shopping and daily life

There is no large-format retail inside the pocket itself — Knollsbrook is settled residential streets, and residents treat that as a feature. The full Barrhaven retail spine — Barrhaven Town Centre and the Marketplace district — sits a few minutes west along Greenbank and Strandherd. One honest buyer note, the same one in the Knollsbrook guide: groceries and a pharmacy will likely need a car from here. And because the pocket is essentially built out, change arrives as infill and renovations rather than new phases — which is part of why its character, and its price band, hold steady year to year.
