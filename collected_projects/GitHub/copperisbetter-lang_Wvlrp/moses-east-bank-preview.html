Beyond East Bank

One wildlife destination. An expanding 3D game world.

East Bank is one part of a much larger vision. WVLRP is building a large, continually evolving interactive 3D online adventure game connected to the real wildlife feeds. Visitors can explore immersive woodland environments, uncover clues and solve ranger mysteries, with more locations, quests and activities in development.

For Moses AutoMall, that creates the potential for natural, memorable automotive-themed discoveries and promotions throughout the wider experience—not just one camera page. These future opportunities are proposed concepts; WVLRP is soft-launched and its full public launch and continuing game expansion are still ahead.

🔑 Imagine the mystery of the missing keys — featuring Moses

Here's a real quest concept from our growing adventure world: the ranger has lost his keys, and visitors follow clues through the woods to track them down. When they find the missing key ring, they discover a Moses AutoMall key fob attached. Recovering the keys helps unlock a previously closed trail. Moses becomes a memorable part of the discovery — not just a logo beside the screen.

Illustrative proposed game integration only. Every destination is open to sponsorship at every tier.

East Bank can welcome small local partners, premier dealership branding, and other approved corporate sponsors together. Sponsorship tier determines how prominently and extensively each brand is promoted — not whether a company can sponsor East Bank. The proposed Moses presenting position would remain the primary page identity.

OPTIONAL FLAGSHIP UPGRADE · TIER 4 · ONLY ONE AVAILABLE

WVLRP also plans an exclusive Tier 4 Discovery Partner: just one company at a time, discoverable across every page, even East Bank and other sponsors' pages. A visitor who explores the 360° world can find that partner in a suitable sign, object, clue or logo. Moses would still retain the dominant East Bank presenting-sponsor presence shown in this proposal. Tier 4 is a separate, higher-level package, not automatically included with an East Bank page sponsorship.

Tier 4 GUARANTEE: Sponsor logos and brand presentation on EVERY WVLRP PAGE — including pages led by other sponsors — and integrated into ALL RELEVANT GAMES AND QUESTS. These are required deliverables for the sole Tier 4 partner, not merely optional appearances. Appropriate creative placements and activation schedule would be agreed by contract.

Proposed Tier 4 investment: $80,000–$120,000 per year ($160,000–$240,000 over two years). A two-year (24-month) sponsorship payment plan is mandatory. Final price, payment schedule, and other terms would be agreed before signing; this is not an active offer or awarded sponsorship.

For a major dealership partnership, Moses can also discuss the exclusive Tier 4 package as a separate sitewide upgrade. The East Bank proposal remains available as its own package.
