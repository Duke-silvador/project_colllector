The project

An open invitation to observe and explore.

West Virginia Live Research Project connects people with the rhythms of local wildlife through field cameras, ongoing observation, and a growing collection of nature-based experiences.

For visitors, it's a place to watch and discover. For partners, it's an opportunity to support a project built around authentic experiences.
