WELCOME TO THE COMMUNITY

Welcome to WVLRP.

Your account is your doorway into the community side of the West Virginia Live Research Project. Watch the land live, follow the wildlife, learn with us, talk with other members, and help the project grow.

📹 The cameras stay free.

WVLRP's live camera feeds are available to the public. You do not have to pay, donate, or buy a membership to watch. The goal is to make a real window into West Virginia wildlife, the property, chickens, projects, and life on the land available to everyone.

💬 Membership opens the community.

Creating your free WVLRP account gives you access to the interactive side of the project. The camera chat rooms require a WVLRP account so we can build a real community and keep conversations manageable and respectful.

  • Participate in camera chats
  • Use your private member inbox
  • Send private messages to other members
  • Keep your own WVLRP notes
  • Follow new cameras, visitors and projects as they are added

🌲 What WVLRP is about.

There is more here than a camera feed. WVLRP follows wildlife, the land, chickens, outdoor projects, education, experiments and a simpler way of life. Watch, learn, share what you see and become part of the story as the project develops.

★ Supporting WVLRP

Membership itself is free. Donations are optional and help keep cameras, streaming, equipment and projects running and allow WVLRP to expand. Supporters may receive additional recognition or access as supporter features and campaigns are offered.

Your ability to watch the public cameras is never dependent on donating.

🏕 Support levels & perks

WVLRP accounts can recognize Members, Donors and Sponsors. Supporter features can include access to the Supporter Lounge and other special project perks when enabled for an account. Sponsorships may also support individual cameras or the project as a whole.

Specific donation offers, gifts and sponsorship packages will be shown on the Support page when available rather than promising a perk that is not currently active.

🛖 The Supporter Lounge

Eligible supporters can enter a separate community space to relax, talk and connect. The Lounge is intended for nature, cameras, music, projects, photos, stories, polls and even some off-topic conversation—while keeping things respectful.

🎮 Select PTZ access

Select supporter tiers may receive Pan-Tilt-Zoom access on compatible cameras, allowing eligible supporters to remotely explore views. PTZ access is tier-specific, availability-dependent, and remains under administrator control.

✉ Your member tools

Your WVLRP account works across the site. Your account menu is where you'll find your Inbox, My Notes, any Supporter Lounge access you receive, and other member features as they are added.

🦌 Latest Visitors

When wildlife shows up, WVLRP can preserve those moments through the visitor side of the site. The cameras aren't just something to leave running in the background—members can help notice, discuss and document what comes through.

🔎 Keep your eyes open.

WVLRP has a playful side too. Hidden details, Easter eggs, special events, contests and other surprises may appear around the site and camera pages. Some things are meant to be discovered rather than announced.

🤝 A few community basics

Enjoy the cameras, respect the wildlife and respect the people watching with you. Don't use chat or private messages to harass other members. Member controls such as blocking are available for private messaging. WVLRP is being built as a place to explore, watch, learn and belong—not as a paywall around nature.
