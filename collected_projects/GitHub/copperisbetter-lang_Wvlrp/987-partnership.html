Beyond The Roost

A living wildlife world. A growing 3D adventure.

98.7 wouldn't simply be joining a chicken camera. WVLRP is building a large, continuously evolving interactive 3D online game connected to its live wildlife experiences. Guests explore immersive woodland scenes, follow trails and uncover ranger mysteries. New adventures, destinations and interactive features are being developed for future releases.

That creates opportunities for featured radio moments, station-inspired challenges and partner discoveries throughout the wider WVLRP world — not just on one page. This is an expanding project in soft launch, not a finished game or a fully launched public platform.

🎵 A station that becomes part of the adventure

Imagine discovering a 98.7 sign on a woodland trail, tuning in to a DJ's clue during a ranger quest, or solving a music challenge tied to the next destination. The station's name, logo and creative tie-ins could appear in relevant games, clues and quests—giving listeners more reasons to explore and return.

Illustrative game integrations, subject to station approval and future development. No exclusive rights to the wider game or The Roost are implied.Tier 2+ sponsorship opportunity: All Tier 2 and higher partners may be considered for story-appropriate branded appearances in selected games and quests. Creative placements, schedule and deliverables are agreed individually; no active placement is implied.
Every destination is open to sponsorship at every tier.

The Roost can welcome small local partners, featured radio branding, and major corporate sponsors together. Sponsorship tier determines the size, prominence and promotional benefits of each placement — not whether a business may sponsor this page. The proposed 98.7 featured position would remain prominent alongside compatible sponsors.

OPTIONAL FLAGSHIP UPGRADE · TIER 4 · ONLY ONE AVAILABLE

Above this featured Roost partnership, WVLRP plans a single Tier 4 Discovery Partner whose brand can be found throughout every page and adventure, including The Roost. On this example, 98.7 would retain its prominent featured radio identity; the global partner would be woven into the environment as a separate, discoverable detail. Tier 4 is a separate exclusive package, not part of this proposed 98.7 agreement.

Tier 4 GUARANTEE: Sponsor logos and brand presentation on EVERY WVLRP PAGE — including pages led by other sponsors — and integrated into ALL RELEVANT GAMES AND QUESTS. These are required deliverables for the sole Tier 4 partner, not merely optional appearances. Appropriate creative placements and activation schedule would be agreed by contract.

Proposed Tier 4 investment: $80,000–$120,000 per year ($160,000–$240,000 over two years). A two-year (24-month) sponsorship payment plan is mandatory. Final price, payment schedule, and other terms would be agreed before signing; this is not an active offer or awarded sponsorship.

For a major partnership, 98.7 can also explore this separate sitewide opportunity instead of, or in addition to, the featured Roost package. The regular Roost proposal stands on its own.
