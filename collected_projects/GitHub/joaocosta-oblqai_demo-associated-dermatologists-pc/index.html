Published numbers

A group that shows its work

On its own employment page the practice publishes its operating numbers: about 32 to 36 patients a day, a 97 to 99 percent net collection rate, and 45 percent operating overhead.

A group confident enough to publish its numbers is a group worth building for. In this concept those figures sit in a proper band on the main page instead of an employment page only the curious ever find.

Access

A portal that stays a portal

The group runs a ModMed patient portal and puts online bill pay in the footer of every page. It books by phone and through the portal rather than a public booking engine, and that is a reasonable way for a practice this size to work.

This concept does not pretend the portal away. It adds one obvious request path on the homepage that hands a guest to the portal or the phone, whichever they came for, and stops making them hunt for either.

What this concept fixes

Three small leaks worth closing

Widget labels in the copy

Stray widget labels leak into the text of the locations page, and a guest reads them as content. In this rebuild the page says only what it means to say.

Nav items going nowhere

Two top nav targets lead to missing pages. Every link in this concept leads somewhere real.

A broken space on error

An error page renders its status with a broken space, which is the kind of detail that makes a whole site feel tired. This concept fixes the space and the page behind it.

Where to start

One clear path from symptom to office

A guest describes what they are dealing with, and the page points them at the right kind of care and the right office, in that order. Medical, surgical, Mohs or cosmetic, the road is the same shape from anywhere in Tucson.
