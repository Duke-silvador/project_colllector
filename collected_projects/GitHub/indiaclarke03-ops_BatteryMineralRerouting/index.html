Why now

The pressure point moved from shipping origin to ownership

The duty lever fell away in March 2026, when the USITC voted against duties. The tax-credit lever stays: US makers lose clean-energy credits if too much of a component's cost comes from a "prohibited foreign entity". So the question is no longer just where the goods shipped from, but who owns the plant they came from.
